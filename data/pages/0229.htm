0229: shaft
La Secada 30T 453251 4798446 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 285m
Length 13m Depth 13m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 12th September 2019; 10th September 2021; 8th January, 4th May 2022; 6th January, 29th April 2024; 14th November 2025

   A small hole by a flat area gives entry to a roomy 13m shaft. (The original description stated 20m). Landing is on boulders with a slight draught although none was detected on the 2025 exploration. These have been dug as its position is directly over Rocky Horror in Cueva Hoyuca (107).
   The site could not be found in August 2019 nor in July and December 2021, April 2022 or December 2023 and January 2024, possibly due to heavy vegetation.

   This shaft may have first been found in 1979 while molephoning - see 1979 summer logbook p35 then p29 and p30. The 1981 BCRA Transactions does not mention "229" but the 1981 logbook, p6 has the "relocation" (and also 249 and 250 - numbers which apparently duplicated #0177 and #0178 when used in the 1981 publication).

   The site was eventually rediscovered, dug out and re-explored in November 2025. A rusty spit sealed the identification.
