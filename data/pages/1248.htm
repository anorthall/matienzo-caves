Updated 12th December 1999; 2nd November 2002; 7th May, 8th October 2007

A small hole in a grass slope with a boulder across the edge. A number of drops slope down to the loose head of a pitch. A 25m drop ends at a 2.5 x 4m area floor with an arch of boulders above a greasy calcite slope. There are a couple of avens and the small passage at the bottom has been dug but is impossibly tight.
