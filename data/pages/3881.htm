3881: cave
Riaño 30T 451221 4800700 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 113m
Length 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A cave entrance a few metres from the track edge and adjacent to the "window" down to the side pool near the Riaño resurgence. This was first spotted several years ago but not recorded. A crawl over boulders for 4m leads to a choke with no obvious way on and no draught.

Reference: anon., 2013b (Easter logbook)
