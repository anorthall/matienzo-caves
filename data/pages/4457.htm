4457: cave
Cobadal 30T 449026 4798303 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 272m
Length 38m Depth 14m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

This site was excavated, explored and documented over 4 days in July 2016, after obtaining permission to dig open a cold store. The dig broke into the the top of an angle-of-rest boulder slope. At the base are three choked holes which draught. Forward, a slippery climb up leads through formations on a taped route. The end is a low dig with a slight draught. However, due to the formations, there is nowhere to put spoil.
