0807: shaft
Mullir 30T 455068 4795421 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 777m
Length 4m Depth 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th May 2001; 3rd May 2004

A crescent-shaped shaft on the uphill side of a complex shakehole which also contains sites 808, 809, 810 and 760. Tagged. Revisited at Easter 2001 when another shaft was "noticed right down in the shakehole on the east wall. A boulder is jammed half way down but is quite passable." This is probably site 760.
The shaft was finally descended at Easter 2004 and found to be 4m deep.

Reference: anon., 1990b (logbook); anon., 2001a (Easter logbook); Corrin Juan, 2003a; anon., 2004b (Easter logbook)
