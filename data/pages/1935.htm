Updated 1st February 2006

An obvious entrance in a small rock outcrop lies behind bushes. A short crawl turns to the right and encounters a 2m climb down to the head of a 5m pitch into a roomy chamber. The hole in the floor chokes and the flatout crawl above this also closes in. Daylight can be seen up a narrow rift on the other side of the chamber, and the corresponding hole on the surface lies to the east on the same outcrop line.
