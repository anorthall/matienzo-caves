The grid reference and altitude are likely to have been taken off a 1:25000 map. The French reference number for this site is SCD2015.

In the small valley which goes up from Colorado in Hoyo del Ramon. Explored October 1988. Choked pit. The site was possibly revisited in October 2004 and described as a climb down into a choked rift about 8m long.
