Description from 2008: A hole by the side of the road under a slab, below a short walled section of bank. There is a passable shaft here for anyone with a strong stomach - the site had various dead animals in it so was not descended.
Ten years later the site was found to be covered with stone slabs and new bags with "fresh death".
