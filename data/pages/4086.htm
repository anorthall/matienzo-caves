   A small cave above the wet weather resurgence of the Torca de los Cañaos - Riocueva system. A 2m climb up enters a small chamber with a rift on the right. Archaeological finds are summarised in Ruiz Cobo J and Muñoz Fernández E, 2013, pp 383-384.
