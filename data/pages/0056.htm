0056: Salamandra, Torca de la (Salamander Pot)
Seldesuto 30T 449898 4794491 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: U) Altitude 400m
Length 50m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 9th May 2023

Two pitches of 5m and 3m lead to a slope of boulders and a squeeze through to a walking sized passage that is soon halted by a boulder choke.
When searched for in April 2023, the probable entrance depression was found to be filled-in with sawn tree trunks. It was thought a tirfor would be required to remove them.

References: anon., 1979 (logbook); Addis F et al, 1979; Corrin J et al, 1981b; Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981; anon., 2023b (Easter logbook)
