0729: Domingo Lopez, Cueva de (Prado de Arriba Casa, Cueva de) (Aro, Cueva del)
San Mames 30T 458310 4800684 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 64m
Length 287m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 4th October 2007; 23rd May 2009; 15th November 2025

A 5x10m passage which appeared to close down after about 80m when first explored. Copper or Bronze Age pottery (Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009) was found on a ledge and in a rift near the end of the cave.
In 2007, the cave was explored up a climb over a large boulder into a continuing large, well decorated passage that ends at a soil choke. A ball of string was discovered part way along - presumably left by the original explorers.

Reference: anon., 1988 (logbook); anon., 2007d (summer logbook); Corrin Juan, 2007a (survey); Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009 (survey, but wrong); anon., 2025c (autumn logbook)
