0813: dig
Cubija 30T 450118 4796851 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 284m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 17th September 2000; 19th September 2017; 9th, 23rd September 2022; 16th May, 20th September 2023; 30th April, 26th September 2024; 11th October 2025; 21st May 2026

   A strongly draughting hole on the wall of a wooded depression. A couple of digging sessions were carried out in 2000 when the draught was good. In August 2017, it was found to be well overgrown and so was not fully investigated. Digging recommenced in August 2022 over four sessions, where a possible route down in the floor was uncovered. Further work occurred over the Easter and summer periods, 2023, and Easter 2024.
   Further work occurred in the summer 2024. In the summer 2025, a strong draught was investigated by removing a large boulder, but the way on was considered to be "a mining job". "It is obviously more than a day or two's work and there was no strong prospect in sight."
   Other draughting holes have been noticed around the depression and it was noticed at the end of March 2026 that a lot of trees and vegetation had been removed from the depression and surroundings.
