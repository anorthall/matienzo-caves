The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

   On the left bank of a small tributary ravine of the Tabladillo valley. The cavity serves as a temporary sink. Crawling descent over 3 or 4 m. Large, low flue (1 m) which expands 5 m further to form a room (8 m x 5) with a 7 m chimney. Squeeze of 2 m and small room of 4 m. A small conduit, to be cleared and visible for a few metres, goes to the left with the temporary flow which runs through the entire cavity.
