5518: depression
Riaño 30T 451733 4801603 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 255m
Length 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   An impressive depression which can most 'easily' be reached by jungle-bashing off the Riaño road just 4m NW of the 30km road sign, marked with red-and-white tape. A more-or-less straight route ends at a vegetated drop into a large cleft, perhaps 6m or more deep. A short rope or ladder climb down reaches a solid floor with boulders. A short horizontal cave about 5 meters long is found under the boulders (formed by the boulders) with no way on except to exit back to the surface. There is nothing in all the brambles except more brambles and dead-end grikes.
