A obvious opening right by the side of the Campo La Cruz - Secadura road. Originally, twenty metres of passage that closed in beyond a short drop. The ADEMCO 2019 Journal, p25-26 has details (survey and photos) of what now appears to be a decent cave, AD-13. They opened it up the year after the MCP inspected it.
