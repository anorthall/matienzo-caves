   A small tube sloping down under a rock outcrop needs digging to drop down a 3m slope into a small chamber. No draught.

   This was explored in February 2023. Only a couple of boulders needed pulling out and a slope enters a passage towards the right. This continues for about 5m to where a rift turns left into the hill. At least 2 or 3m long, it was too narrow to follow. [Pete Smith]
