4985: cave
Las Calzadillas 30T 447385 4794937 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 535m
Length 5m Depth 2m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   The entrance slopes down on leaves for 2m then levels out for 3m to boulders on the left with a turn to the right, leading after 1.5m to a very low airspace continuation. At the corner a narrow rising rift to the left is similar to site 4987 and may link to it. [Site 14a; Phil Parker]
