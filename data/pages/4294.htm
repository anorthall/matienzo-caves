Length 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 25th September 2018; 17th November 2022

The information below has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club Dijon reference, Simonnot G, 2016.

Five meters to the left of resurgence site 4293. A small 3m tube has been cleared of its clay. A continuation, looking back to 4293 is visible for about 5 m. Despite the lack of air flow, the cavity is probably related to the nearby Cueva del Tocayo.
The cave was worked on in 2007.
