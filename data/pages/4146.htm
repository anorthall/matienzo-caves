4146: cave
Navajeda 30T 444690 4801160 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: A) Altitude 64m
Length 10m Depth 2m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th January 2018

Three entrances explored on a levelled area, down valley from Famous Five Cave. Although all are within 20m of each other, on the scar, there is no obvious connection between them.

A partially excavated entrance on the right of the rock face, 2m deep with 9m of passage. The site ends in a small, decorated chamber, with no way out.

The sites close by are 4144 and 4145. The positions of sites 4146 and 4145 were altered January 2018 to more accurately show their position on the cliff line.

Reference: anon., 2015b (Easter logbook)
