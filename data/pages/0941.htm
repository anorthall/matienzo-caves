Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

About 100m up-valley from site 834, round a rocky outcrop and near a ruined barn. The entrance is in an overgrown depression and has a K carved in the rock just above the excavated entrance.

The tight shaft is a 2m drop and 5m climb down followed by a small loose 10m pitch. Part way down is a small slot which has a draught but which is too small.
