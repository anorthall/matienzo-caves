La Secada 30T 451888 4797811 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 232m
Length 3m Depth 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th, 22nd, 31st January 2024

A small fissure under a tree has been enlarged to a 3m pitch to a 10cm crack with a strong draught.

By December 2023, the tree had grown to quite a size but had been uprooted. The entrance was dug open and the 3m climb descended to check the dig. "It looks like a reasonable prospect but would be quite some effort requiring a bucket and rope to pull the spoil up to the surface." The site lies over the end chamber of Cueva Germán, site 1232.
