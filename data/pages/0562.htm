0562: Sandstone Pot
El Naso
Length 12m Depth 12m
Logbook search

On the uphill side of a very large depression. A 12m deep pot lands on sandstone blocks with an impossible eyehole through the wall where stones drop for about 12m. The cave echoes well but there is no draught.

Reference: anon., 1985b (logbook)
