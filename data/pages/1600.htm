Updated 30th December 2000; 23rd April 2001; 21st May 2026

A couple of large boulders cover the top of this roomy shaft. A ledge is met about 14m down and the landing is on a choked floor at -22m. A tight rift leads to the top of a 7m undescended drop. The 3m long rift requires enlarging. There may be a passage up a rift on the pitch but this has not been investigated.

The site was seen in April 2026 but was not descended as it was heavily overgrown.
