4955: sink
Las Piqueras 30T 445706 4794507 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 609m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd May 2023

   A site identified as a wet weather sink taking a significant amount of water and a possible site for water tracing to help assess the extent of the catchment for Fuente Aguanaz. In April 2023, it was found that a car could drive up to get within 100 - 150m of the site.
