4420: Iglesia 2, Cueva de la
Navajeda 30T 443828 4801675 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 54m
Length 11m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th October 2016; 24th May 2017

   The site had a number of bones and teeth excavated and itemised in Ruiz Cobo J and Muñoz Fernández E, 2013
   A low and wide entrance in a rock face near the road. It quickly branches into two short, low passages with no obvious way on.
   At Easter 2017, the left hand passage was dug to a low choked chamber, the right became too tight. The site does not warrant further digging.
(Description by John Southworth)
