4252: cave
Barrio de Arriba 30T 444723 4799536 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 165m
Length 12m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   The entrance is in a low cliff face to a 12m long crawling passage with daylight entering in two places. This ends at an eyehole looking into a small chamber which would require capping. Not a good prospect; no draught.
(Description: John Southworth)
