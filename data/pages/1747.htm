The grid reference and altitude are likely to have been taken off a 1:25000 map. The French reference number for this site is SCD2003.

Close to the last houses to the northeast of Arredondo, it is the source of a small tributary to the Rio Bustablado first exploration in August 1988. A small tube with a tiny stream soon splits into two: a watery conduit seems to sump and an inactive branch is impenetrable after a few meters.
