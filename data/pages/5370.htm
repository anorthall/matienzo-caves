Length 15m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A body-sized, steeply sloping entrance under a hazel tree at the edge of a field and woodland. A small chamber is met with a mixture of limestone and sandstone blocks making up the floor. To the left, the chamber drops slightly to a small sink. Two, relatively active bats present: greater horseshoe and lesser horseshoe.
