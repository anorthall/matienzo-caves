2590: White Chamber Cave
S Vega 30T 450917 4794142 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 657m
Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 15th October 2011

A collapsed hole in clints to the east of a closed depression. At the back is a dig with a possible inward draught. In the middle is a crawl to a calcite chamber. The hole needs work to reach a possible continuation and would be better looked at in warmer weather.

In 2011, the hole was forced to a small (2m) continuation with no draught felt. "No likely further prospects."

Reference: anon., 2006e (autumn logbook); anon., 2011d (summer logbook)
