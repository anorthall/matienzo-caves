Updated 27th April 2012

"Below a tree and above an obvious hole on the hillside, this is an open cave that needs investigating."

At Easter 2012, the entrance was found hidden by a fallen tree. A flat out crawl enters a chamber with a calcited slope up to the right and a trench down on the left which is completely blocked by a large rock.

Reference: anon., 2006b (Easter logbook); anon., 2012b (Easter logbook)
