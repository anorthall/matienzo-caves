Updated 22nd May 2014; 1st May 2024

   An grassy hole to a rift which appeared be 5 to 10m deep and required de-nettling.

   It was cleared and investigated in March 2023. The drop turned out to be 2m, to a slot. The muddy slot was easily opened up and led to an easy 2m climb into a chamber that is 1.6m across and about 2m high. The roof here too was made out of mud, spiders and slugs. There is a way on - a low crawl with a solid rock roof about 3 inches high with a mud floor. It should be easy to dig at 20°N, but there's no draught.
