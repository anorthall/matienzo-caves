0633: Casa de los Cristales, La Cueva de la (Otero III, Cueva) (Tío Vidal, Cueva del)
Secadura 30T 457325 4800123 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 50m
Length 20m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th November 2004; 16th May 2009; 25th June 2010

Walking size cave the entrance of which contains a level with flints and bone fragments, probably Palaeolithic. Reference Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009 has a summary of the Palaeolithic and Mesolithic deposits.
