3693: cave
Moncobe 30T 446290 4796363 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 240m
Length 24m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 30th November 2012; 24th May 2017

On the west side of the valley up into a re-entrant with evergreen trees. A small cliff face on the left with boulders has a small hole down giving access to a 3m climb down. The cave slopes down for 6m then up for 8m over calcite to a small chamber.

At the top of the calcite slope at the end of the cave, a dig at floor level gained access to about 4m of passage heading down and back towards the base of the slope. All ways choke.

At Easter 2017, the site was found to be draughting well but no further way on could be found.

Reference: anon., 2012b (Easter logbook); anon., 2012e (autumn logbook); anon., 2017b (Easter logbook)
