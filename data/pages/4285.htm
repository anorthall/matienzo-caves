Updated 25th September 2018; 17th November 2022

The information below has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club Dijon reference, Simonnot G, 2016.

The steep flanked doline (-5m) is pierced with a p5 followed by an inclined slab. Below, a relatively spacious room (15-20 m long) runs into a pool with water supply which seems to correspond to a sinkhole on the other side of the road.
A rubbish tip. The descent is "so pleasant" among the carcasses of washing machines or vacuum cleaners and bags containing some unsavory items.
Explored by the SC Dijon, August 2006.
