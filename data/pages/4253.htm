   An undescended shaft of about 9m depth. Another one, 4m away, looks too tight. The shaft was explored at Easter 2016 and found to be a 6m deep rift shaft.  One end of rift is a chamber which chokes. The opposite end is a squeeze to a descending rift which also chokes.
(Description: John Southworth)
