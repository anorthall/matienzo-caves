The information below has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club Dijon reference, Simonnot G, 2016.

A nice entrance, 2.5m x 2.6m gives access to a large chamber of 10m diameter and five meters high at an aven. An attempt has been made to clear a side tube, but it is well blocked by the work of badgers. The lack of air flow is not encouraging.
Excavated and survey by the SC Dijon, April 2009.
