4611: cave
Garzón, Solórzano 30T 449942 4803443 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 286m
Length 25m Depth 20m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance is adjacent to open pasture. A small, crawl-in cave, which opens up into a 40 degree hading passage, 20m in length. The end is boulder filled, but there are good formations in the lower part.
