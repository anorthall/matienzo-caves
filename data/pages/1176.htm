The system was resurveyed during the Easter 2000 expedition. The lengths and depths mentioned have the original Spanish data in brackets.

A Catalan site which may be a future top entrance to SVS/Vallina system. The stream passage ends 306m (340m) down at a feature which could be removed. An alternative grid reference from Cantabria Subterránea. Catálogo de las Grandes Cavidades (García José León, 1997) is VN48799369 z650 (datum?).

The boulders blocking the route at the base of the cave have a couple of Hilti cap holes.
