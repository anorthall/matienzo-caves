Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd October 2009; 12th September 2014

In a wooded shakehole in the middle of a field. A boulder slope leads down to a climb into a chamber. A low passage on the right has a crawl to a choke downstream and an aven upstream. A high level passage reached by a 3m climb from the chamber leads to a crawl which was dug to an aven with a possible passage at the top, and a further passage becoming too tight.

References: Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981; anon., 1987 (logbook); material in file; anon., 1992a (Easter logbook)
