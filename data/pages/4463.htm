4463: Iglesia 1, Cueva de la (Navajeda, Cueva de)
Navajeda 30T 443806 4801654 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 57m
Length 151m Depth 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   A notable entrance, up from the field and in dense trees. A slope down into the entrance is followed by a low crawl down into a chamber. Leading off are a number of short climbs and squeezes, all red mud coated. The cave has a small window to the outside to the south.
    The site has been the scene of archaeological investigations since 1922 and many artefacts have been catalogued. The explorations and survey of 2016 extended the cave to the south beyond the old Spanish survey.

References: Ruiz Cobo J and Muñoz Fernández E, 2013 (survey); anon., 2016c (summer logbook)
