The information has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club de Dijon reference, Degouve de Nuncques Patrick et Simonnot Guy, 1989 (in Sous le Plancher, no 4, 1989). The French reference number for this site is SCD2023).

To the south-east of Hoyo de Llaneces (site 1752) and explored in April 1988.
This abrupt collapse doline in the sandstones holds a luxuriante vegetation along the walls which obscures it. Animals maintain a path which goes down to the bottom  where, under an overhang, there is natural feeding trough. No continuation was seen.

Simonnot G, 2022 gives a position of 30T 452403 4792106 (ETRS89)
