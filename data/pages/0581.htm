A cave opened up uphill from Cueva de Riaño (105). There are two low level passages where a roof passage follows over the top of the right hand one. The grid reference was checked with GPS at Easter 2006, entrances photos taken in the summer and the cave surveyed and photographed at Easter 2007. (Not yet drawn up - see computer folder).
