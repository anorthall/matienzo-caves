2816: shaft
La Secada 30T 450918 4797947 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 405m
Length 9m Depth 7m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 18th April 2008

A wide shaft drops 5m to a boulder and soil floor. On the west side there is a small alcove with animal bones and on the east side, a 2m climb down leads to a narrow rift which continues round a corner. It should be possible to enlarge the corner although it appears to pinch in according to a brightened image.
It would appear that the boulder and soil floor was part of the roof to a chamber, recently collapsed.
