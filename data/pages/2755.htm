2755: sinks
La Vega 30T 450790 4795715 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 165m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A couple of sinks located in the bed of the river, noted in 2006 during the summer dry period and visited with Juan Carlos Fernández Gutiérrez during the summer 2007, when tiny "whirlpools" were seen. The farmer tries to keep the sinks choked so that water flows to the rest of the land towards Cueva del Molino.
