[New grid reference, December 2023]
A small, draughting entrance is a 2m squeeze down into a small, unstable boulder chamber. A steep 5m slope drops to the sloping head of a 15m pitch which appears very loose where the ladder hangs free and is hence undescended.
Despite the above description, the site was at least partly explored in July 2025. There is no logbook account but there are some photos, below.

Probably joined to Sima de la Piluca (0472).
