0726: Charcas, Cueva de las
El Naso 30T 451957 4796560 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 336m
Length 88m Depth 7m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 20th June 2021; 11th September, 3rd November 2021; 8th January 2022

   The entrance may be difficult to find being a flat out crawl into a small chamber - the grid reference was updated in January 2022. Beyond a stal grill lies the main chamber, which is well decorated and contains cave pearls. At the end, two routes lead into the final chamber. The site was resurveyed for a centre line in the Spring, 2021 and photographed the following August and December.
