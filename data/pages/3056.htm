La Collada 30T 448130 4799363 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 385m
Length 50m Depth 40m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2013

First discovered at Easter, 2008 but not recorded. The draughting rift is in a small depression and goes 5m to a drop of perhaps 10m. The site drops between boulders and through some small chambers for a total of about 40m depth.
At Easter, the site was mentioned as having an inaccurate location but the GPS reading was almost the same as that given above. It was also re-explored but no good digging places were found.

Reference: anon., 2008e (summer logbook); anon., 2013b (Easter logbook)
