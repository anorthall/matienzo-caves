  One of a set of three holes just below the track not far from Torca de Azpilicueta, the other being sites 4802 and 4804. Immediately to the west of #4802. This 5m long rift is fenced with 7 metal posts and barbed wire. It drops 3m to where the way on is too narrow and choked..
