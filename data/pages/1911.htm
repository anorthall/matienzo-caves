Length 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 1st February 2006; 20th December 2008

   A crouching-sized, horizontal tube in a triangular cliff face shelter with no draught. The cave is 2m diameter and quite wet inside; 2m from the entrance it turns left and stops after 8m. There are a large number of rusty sardine cans, so possibly a Civil War shelter. Further illustrated information about the Civil War in the area can be found here.
