1380: dig
La Secada 30T 451148 4797281 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 299m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 13th September 2021; 21st May 2026

The entrance is in a field with other boulder piles which conceal holes. A small black hole can be seen. The site draughts and would make a good dig. Howver, in August 2021, a team "could not find anything of interest".

In April 2026, the hole was found covered with boulders with a good draught. The way-on was found about 1 excavated metre down beneath a fridge sized calcite boulder. It was a descending slot approximately 4" wide amongst clean washed rocks. The dig appeared long term, so the team re-filled the hole.

Reference: anon., 1999a (Easter logbook); anon., 2021c (summer logbook); anon., 2026a (Easter logbook)
