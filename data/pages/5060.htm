5060: Basura, Torca de la (LC186)
Cierrolinos 30T 449009 4804716 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 320m
Length 20m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 11th November 2021; 23rd May 2022; 27th February 2023

A very steep ramp that ends in a meander giving way to a small room where the air is lost through an impenetrable hole.
When explored in April 2022, it was noted, just before the final room, there are two high level possibilities which are probably the source of the draught.
(The original photo attached to this site is, in fact, a hole located about 20m to the southwest. It has been coded as 5183.)
MCP photos from April 2022 were added February 2023.
