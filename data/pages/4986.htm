   Some meters above 4985, a 2m high slot in a rock face is just too narrow to enter but larger inside. The floor slopes downwards in a narrow section to a constriction, possibly continuing but would need examination by capping. There is a cool gentle outward draught. Immediately to the left of the slot, at foot level, a low downward sloping undercut has been probed to a length of 2m, possibly continuing. [Site 14b; Phil Parker]
