2575: Dingle View Cave
El Naso 30T 451424 4796214 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: A) Altitude 320m
Length 18m Height +4
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd May 2014; 16th, 21st September 2021

   Visible passage to the east of Cueva de Coberruyo (site 0138). It was given the name when first explored in 2021. A 3m back-and-foot climb leads into the left cave, which is actually two caves. The left hand cave is about 5m long and, across the climb up, is the right hand cave. This is about 12m long before it gets too narrow and appears to continue very small. [Alex Ritchie]
