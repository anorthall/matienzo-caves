The shaft entrance into Cueva Coquisera, 0039.
The following description comes from pp20-21 of the report of the 2025 Campaign by the Colectivo Piezo, linked below.
   As the second entrance to Codisera, it is relatively little known. It consists of a 42m shaft, divided into two sections of ramps containing loose rocks. Shortly into the descent, several high openings can be seen letting in daylight—perhaps where its name comes from.
    At the bottom, we found the access point that reaches the white flowstone of the '400 Pesetas Passage.' A small but constant flow of water feeds this formation. From the other section, after a small 3m drop, you enter a high, narrow, and highly polished meander, which provides access to the intermittent lake in the '400 Pesetas Passage.'
   There is a photo looking up the shaft on page 21.
