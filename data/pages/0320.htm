0320: shaft

Length 27m Depth 27m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 18th April 1999; 1st April 2001

Previously an "undescended shaft of about 90m depth" although the 1992 account has this hole free-climbed to 4m and a draughtless choke! At Easter 99 the hole was relocated as a 4m climb down to the hidden head of a substantial pitch where a bolt was placed.
The shaft top was opened up and descended in 2001 where the main pitch was found to be a 23m deep, circular shaft.
