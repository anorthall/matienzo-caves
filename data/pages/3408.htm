San Antonio 30T 445747 4801703 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 105m
Length 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th November 2010

A low entrance in the side of a shakehole is followed immediately by an awkward wriggle to the right and a 3m descent of a small shaft to a choked floor. At the entrance, a very narrow vertical slot, around 2m or so deep, had a slight draught and the floor appeared to slope away to the left. Slot would require opening up to allow access to normal thin people.
