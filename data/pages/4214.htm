A large depression, with exposed limestone high up on the uphill side, has two possible digs in the base. This site is the western hole which has a good draught. The hole has been partly excavated revealing boulders and increasingly larger, open spaces. "Needs a return." The other hole is site 4213.
Site 4214 had "slumped in somewhat" when seen in December 2023.
