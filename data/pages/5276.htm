The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

   In the southern part of the Garma de Piqueras. To get there you have to climb from Bustablado in the direction of Cullaherboso and Garma Blanca passing through Tabladillo.
    The entrance at ground level is 2.5 x 1.4 m. The torca consists of a single 31 m deep pitch. At the bottom it is completely filled with stones. From the middle of the pitch, the dimensions are enlarged and you end up in a small sloping room. There is no continuation possible at the bottom (no air circulation).
    At the time of the descent in May, a rook (and her three eggs) nested in the middle of the pitch.
