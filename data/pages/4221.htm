4221: Reñada, Cueva-Cubío de la (top entrance)
S Vega 30T 450341 4795506 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: A) Altitude 206m
Length see the top entrance to the South Vega System, Torca de Azpilicueta
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 24th May 2017; 30th June 2018; 10th January 2020

The upper entrance to the Reñada cave system. It is located at the end of a faint path that climbs the hillside, starting where the small stream is crossed. All descriptions and photos (except for entrance photos below) are found in site 0048.
Various species of bats have been observed underground in this entrance.
