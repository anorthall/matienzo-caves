3151: cave
El Suto 30T 450152 4800183 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 210m
Length 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance to site 3151 is in a walled enclosure and leads over debris into a hands-and-knees height chamber / passage. A row of stal down the centre leads to where the floor slopes up to a flat out crawl and possible continuation. There may also be a way off to the left. There is a slight outward draught.
