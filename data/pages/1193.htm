Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The site is an unexplored shaft in the lowest shakehole in the hanging valley on the north side of the Llueva valley, close to its junction with the San Miguel valley. In the summer of 1996, a small bouldery top was guarded by a rotting corpse. Stones drop about 7m.
