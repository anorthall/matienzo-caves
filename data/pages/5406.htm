   A vertical slot in limestone initially filled in with rubble. Stones were removed to show the slot continues back approx. 1.5 - 2.0m with a window over the top of the remaining rubble showing the cave may continue. The hole needs capping as it's too narrow to dig further. The slot is two fists wide, shaped like mainland UK and has been left walled up.
