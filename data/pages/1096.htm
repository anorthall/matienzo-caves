Updated 6th May, 3rd November 2009; 22nd May 2017

In a pit below the track. A 2m square entrance lies at the base of a 4m high cliff. A cool cave, explored down a 4m pitch to a slope down of about 5m to an undescended, narrow p5 in summer 2009. The position was moved when site 1095 was GPS'ed, Easter 2009. (The hole was refound and wrongly named as site 3255 and 4504 for short times.)
