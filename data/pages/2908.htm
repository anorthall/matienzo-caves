Updated 15th October 2011; 18th May 2023

(No description). Not found in 2011.

   The cave was located in April 2023 when "Orux Maps took us pretty much straight there". There is an obvious depression at the top side of a large gorse / shrub area with the entrance at the base of a small rock outcrop.
    A snug entrance slopes down steeply over earth / mud and agricultural bags to a sit-up area. A short climb down over flowstone enters a rift passage. Another squeeze drops through to the bottom of a pretty rift passage. (Bang wire was found where earlier progression was being attempted down at this point.) There is a climb up into higher passage which was carried out.
[Jess Eades / Martyn Grayson]
