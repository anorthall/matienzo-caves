Length 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance lies at the base of a 5m high limestone scar in a wooded depression that also contains the entrance to site 2488. A squeeze down over a large block enters a roomy "chamber". An excavated slope leads to a higher level and a boulder choke with the draught coming up from below.
