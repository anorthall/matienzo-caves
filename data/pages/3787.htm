These holes are found above Lenny’s Cave (site 3721) and are all very close together.
There's a track heading across and up the hill which starts in the woods near the corner of the field below site 3741. All of the shafts are just below this track, some are really digs. It's as though there's a joint on which several shafts have developed, which could even be the back end of the boulder chokes in Lenny's Cave and site 0289 next to it. The GPS'd site has been descended. This drops a couple of metres in a slot to some trapped boulders which need removing. Three to four metres further is visible. The rest need work before they can be descended.
