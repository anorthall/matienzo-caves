Length 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 9th October 2013; 5th October 2015; 7th January 2018

Three digs all within 10m of each other at streambed level. All have been dug for about 3m in length but end in large hanging death boulders with no sign of solid walls or roof. All have a very strong draft at times. (An alternative easting is ETRS89: 0446293). A smoke test from here to cavers in El Cubillón was aborted in 2013 when the site was found draughting out.
