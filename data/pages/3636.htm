3636: cave
El Suto 30T 450496 4800126 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 237m
Length 6m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th May 2016; 20th January 2026

The biggest, eastern entrance in a series of small holes (site 3637). This is a stooping size cave to a choke. At the start of the cave is an undescended pit that needs a ladder.
The site (and 3637) could not be found in December 2025 due to thick vegetation including 2m high brambles.
