3174: Pooch Cave
Hornedo 30T 448475 4800993 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 111m
Length 40m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th October 2013; 25th September 2014; 21st May 2023

A 2m high passage continues over an 8m deep pitch to a small chamber with a 3m pitch in the floor. In 2013, the first pitch was descended to a possible rift heading back towards the entrance, but it would need enlarging to progress. The 3m drop was found to be choked.
When the high level was revisited in April 2023, the site was found to be draughting well.

Reference: anon., 2009a (Easter logbook); Corrin Juan, 2010; anon., 2013d (summer logbook); anon., 2023b (Easter logbook)
