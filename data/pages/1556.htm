1556: cave
El Naso 30T 451632 4796778 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 346m
Length 15m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 17th, 22nd January 2008; 4th November 2009

The cave entrance lies to the northwest of Sima Fiesta (68) and is shown on the survey of that pot in the 1975 publication. The entrance leads to a low crawl into the back of the cave. The site has been used as a cow shelter.

Reference: Kendal Caving Club and Manchester University Speleological Society, 1975 (survey); Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981; anon., 2000c (Summer logbook); anon., 2008a (January 2008 logbook); anon., 2008c (Easter logbook); anon., 2009c (summer logbook)
