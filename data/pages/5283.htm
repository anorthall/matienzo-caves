The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

     In the southern part of the Garma de Piqueras. To get there you have to climb from Bustablado towards Cullaherboso and Garma Blanca passing through Tabladillo.
    Entrance of 2.3 x 1.2 m. This is a simple shaft of 20 m without continuation at the bottom. At mid-pitch and to the bottom, we see a narrow gap which suggests a parallel shaft without much interest. There is no draught.
