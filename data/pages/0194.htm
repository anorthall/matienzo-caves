0194: Horses Head Cave (2404 (French: SCD))
Alisas 30T 447488 4793581 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 640m
Length 30m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 21st September 2018; 14th November 2022

A rift at the side of the road. A climb down is followed immediately by a 6m pitch and a traverse over a large block. The passage then doglegs to the right and becomes very tight in a descending, draughting rift. It's been found to draught on a warm day.

References: anon., 1978 (logbook); anon., 1980a (logbook); Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981; anon., 2001c (Summer logbook); Simonnot G, 2018; Simonnot G, 2022
