A tight entrance pitch of 10m drops into a 1x1m streamway on sandstone which becomes too tight and awkward downstream and closes in upstream. The cave needs surveying and pushing.
At Easter 2006, a lower entrance (site 2563) at a stream sink was found and, at the time, it was thought that that was another entrance to this site.
