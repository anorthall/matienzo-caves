0284: Cubillo 3, Surgencia (C3 (MCP 1976)) (Cagulía 1 (LBT))
Ogarrio 30T 456148 4793221 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 130m
Length 37m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 3rd November 2021; 22nd January 2026

   A resurgence with length variously reported as 37m or 60m until a pool is reached. The entrance was found dry in November 2025 and the red painted "Cagulía-1" noted inside the entrance

    An update to the documentation for some of the caves and resurgences in this area took place at the end of 2025. See sites 4275, 5503, 5504, 5505, 5506 and 5507

  First looked at in 1976 by the MCP, the caves have been explored by the F.C.M.E. (ref: BV) and Lobetum (ref: BVc). The original notes for the area described a wet weather resurgence, still going but more suitable clothing needed and that other caves nearby needed pushing.

   The sites have no description in reference BV, just a survey in some cases:

  • Site 16: Cueva Cagulia no survey, no length. ?
  • Site 17: S. Cubillo 1 no survey, no length (#5507)
  • Site 18: not named, survey, length 30m
  • Site 19: S. Cubillo 3, survey, length 60m (#0284)

   The sketch surveys in L76 should "agree" with the BV surveys in some cases. Not all the sites noted in 1976 have been documented in BV. "C3" in logbook 1976 is this site 0284. A Lobetum document produced a couple of years later (BVc) has more information but is not yet online.

   In November / December 2015 water tracing from Cueva Orillón was found not to emerge in this area. Detectors were placed in this resurgence (Surgencia Cubillo 3, that the AEC Lobetum catalogue [1995?] as site 1) and a resurgence further to the north catalogued by the Cuencans as site 5. The Surgencia Cubillo 3 has been GPS'd at 30T 0456164 4793211 but the above GPS was found OK at the end of 2025.
