3886: sink
Ozana 30T 453813 4794440 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: A) Altitude 270m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd May 2015

(The grid reference is an estimate). Water coming out of site 3885 sinks at this point. Heavy rainfall produces a cascade down the hillside that can be seen in a photo of the 0246 area.

In March 2015, 300ml of OBA was put into the sink. Detectors were placed in site 3884 and Jivero 1. The latter proved positive.

Reference: anon., 2013b (Easter logbook); anon., 2015b (Easter logbook)
