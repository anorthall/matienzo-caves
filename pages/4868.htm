4868: Rincón, Cueva del
El Rincón 30T 451416 4805559 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 109m
Length 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A small cave which has archaeological interest. Human bones were encountered while digging in a number of places for a cave extension in April 2019. They have been left in situ and digging activity suspended.

References: Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009 (survey); anon., 2019b (Easter logbook)
