0291: cave
Secadura 30T 455348 4799321 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 99m
Length 15m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 21st April 2013

An interesting cold hole at the lower end of a blind valley above Los Boyones (117), the main resurgence in Secadura. A small rift in the floor is choked; a small chamber above this also appears to be choked.
The GPS taken at Easter 2013 is ETRS89: 30T 455330 4799307, probably putting it in the "wrong place".

References: anon., 1981a (logbook); Corrin J et al, 1981a; Corrin J, 1983c; anon., 2013b (Easter logbook)
