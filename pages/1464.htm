1464: cave
Llueva 30T 454618 4798621 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 304m
Length 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 3rd May 2004; 10th October 2007

A walk-down rift in a large depression with a part cut down chestnut tree. There are slabs covering a hole in the floor, with another hole over a sand bank to the right. At Easter 2004, the site was revisited to reveal nothing under the slabs but up to the left a climb into a small chamber with no prospects.
In 2007, the grid reference was altered from ETRS89: 30T 454768 4798651 (VN54879886) to the above, under the most southern of 3 trees although it could be under the most westerly one!
