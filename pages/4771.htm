4771: shaft
Las Calzadillas 30T 447426 4795051 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 494m
Length 10m Depth 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 25th July 2019

The opening was enlarged with both hammer and chisel and then a snapper in June 2019. An 8m descent in a rift to a floor of boulders, earth and leaves. To one side a visible 3m drop in a narrow cleft needs work to get down it. Stones rattle down the cleft for two or three seconds but it sounds as if there is no increase in width.

References: anon., 2018b (Easter logbook); anon., 2019c (Whit logbook)
