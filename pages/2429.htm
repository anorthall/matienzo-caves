2429: Side Shaft Cave
Ozana 30T 454449 4795189 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 417m
Length 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

This cave is found at the base of the cliff on a ledge. The cave is accessed by traversing along the ledge from the west. It's entered by climbing 4 metres up into the cave; the entrance rises to 8 metres and short horizontal development gives 4 metres length.
