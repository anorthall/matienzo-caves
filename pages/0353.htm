Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 8th, 16th January, 2nd July 2022

Original description: An undescended shaft with a possible draught. About 15m deep.

   The entrance was opened up in December 2021 to reveal a 2 x 1m shaft, 8m deep to a 20cm slot and visible passage. The slot was enlarged but the way on at the base turned out to be 10cm wide for at least 3.5m.
