Two holes next to each other that have not been properly investigated. The western hole is a steep, grass-sided depression with a possible hole at the base; the eastern site is a hole by a limestone face almost completely filled with a small holly bush. The holes have earthy bottoms. Slightly higher up the hill are a couple of tiny draughting holes.
