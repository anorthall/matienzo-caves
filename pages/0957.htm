Updated 18th January 2004; 11th October 2025; 23rd January 2026

Shown in Actividades Regionales. Exploraciones en Cantabria (anon., 1993a) as site 21. The draughting cave splits and becomes too small.

Later work has extended the cave up a couple of short pitches and climbs that pop out near the top of a big 'meander' rift passage about 9m high and 1.5m wide. There are some well decorated chambers and pitches to drop at both ends of the passage.
