5419: Plant Pot Cave
Las Calzadillas 30T 448264 4795138 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 447m
Length 6m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

  A cave entrance in a sandstone band behind the recently renovated barn. The cave is about 6m long, breathing at the end, but with no clear way on. It was revealed by the land owner while renovating the barn. The cave is on private land, only accessible by going through the garage area, so permission must be sought from the land owner, Fernando.
