A small cave entrance lies above the track. A short crawl meets a junction. To the left, a squeeze through into a rift lands on the top of a narrow shaft, about 7m deep and covered with blocks that need removing. To the right, a squeeze through at the end of 5m of stooping-height rift passage enters a section of tiny tubes.
On the right of the entrance, a tiny hole is at the top of a small shaft which is about 10m deep. This may dig.
