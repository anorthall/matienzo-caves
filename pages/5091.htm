Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 12th November 2021

  An open entrance hidden behind vegetation at the north side of the Coquisera (0039) entrance. A hands-and-knees crawl lead to a constriction with the continuation seen ahead. The soft sides of the constriction were removed in October, 2021 but the roomy crawl closed in at a tiny hole in mud. There appears to be a faint draught.
