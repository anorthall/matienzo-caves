A 2x1.5m oval pitch increasing to 5m across a metre or so down and capped with a deliberately placed stone. Passage can be seen leading off and the floor is visible about 13m down. The site is choked with no draught, and it is unlikely to be a fruitful dig.
Another smaller rift-like shaft about 10m southwest at same level and yet another a few metres to the northeast. Neither has been opened up as they
do not look to have potential and they would be difficult to close up to prevent livestock falling in.
