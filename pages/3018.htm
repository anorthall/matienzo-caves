This narrow shaft is found in the top corner of the wood near Slaves' Cave and Freedom Pot. It was explored in 2013 down a very tight shaft into a chamber. A drop enters a 20m long, decorated passage that finishes in a tight rift.

Reference: anon., 2008e (summer logbook); anon., 2013d (summer logbook)
