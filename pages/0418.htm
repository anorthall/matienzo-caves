Updated 28th November 2023

   Originally described as an undescended pot of about 25m depth on an open limestone exposure and probably rediscovered and descended at Easter 2004.
A 3m long slot in flat ground 7m south of a double depression with trees on each side. A 14m deep, clean -washed, blind shaft.

   During a search of the hillside in October 2023, the shaft was descended before realising that it was a known site.
