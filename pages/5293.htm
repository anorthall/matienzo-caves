The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

   Following the violent rains of the last 3 weeks, a collapse has formed in the ditch bordering the track connecting Arredondo to Llaneces. The one metre deep sink, with a part limestone wall on the road side, serves as a loss for a newly created temporary flow. The loss was filled by the road services at the beginning of January 2022. Collapsed after new rains, it was filled again in March 2022, then in April 2022…..
