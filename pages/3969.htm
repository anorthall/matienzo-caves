3969: cave
Barrio la Mina 30T 444642 4800145 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 215m
Length 15m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th May 2016; 8th January 2018

An overhanging wet and dark sandstone outcrop in the middle of a gully and just below a forestry track. There is a good draught coming out from below the outcrop and there are numerous rocks which may have been placed in the site some time ago. A couple of hours digging gave about 3m of access. Another visit and dig opened 5m along the face to the northwest, the opposite direction.
On a final vist over Easter 2014, the slot on the right was followed to a boulder. Various contortions eventually allows entry to a flat-roofed chamber 7m x 2m x 1.5m high. No way on was found from here. In 2016, the site was "not pursued".
