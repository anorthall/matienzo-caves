Located in a wooded depression near a farm building. The entrance was excavated for 3m to a tight pitch head, leading to 15m drop down a fine shaft, unfortunately blocked at the bottom. Several windows and a parallel shaft were entered on the way back up, all ending too tight. A visit back at another time to see if it is strongly draughting would encourage a return to dig.
