1270: cave

Length 30m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 7th May 2007

A short cave entered from behind the boulder pile associated with the pumping complex leads via a low wet passage to a draughting boulder choke with no apparent way on. The whole cave would flood in wet weather and must be associated with Los Boyonnes (117).

Reference: anon., 1997b (logbook)
