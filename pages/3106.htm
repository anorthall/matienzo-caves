3106: cave
San Antonio 30T 445451 4800800 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 147m
Depth 2m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th October 2013; 19th April 2016

   A 2m drop down a muddy slope onto sandstone boulders that choke the top of the shaft. At that point you can look across through boulders to an opening that requires digging. In the summer 2013, it appeared to need a lot of work to clear back the vegetation to get to the entrance.
    The site draughts but it needs careful digging with a shovel to progress. Loose boulders.
