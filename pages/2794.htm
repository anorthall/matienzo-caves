2794: Hazel Cave
Secadura 30T 454275 4799377 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 342m
Length 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A difficult to find cave at the base of a small scar behind a hazel tree and bracken. A low entrance leads to a 5m long sideways crawl which has not been explored. The end cannot be seen. There was a small outward draught on a summers day and there is a small flow of water.

Reference: anon., 2007d (summer logbook); Corrin Juan, 2007a
