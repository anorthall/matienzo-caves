Length 32m Depth 12m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 16th October 2005; 4th November 2021

   A 12m roomy shaft drops down a pronounced rift. At the bottom, a duck under the northern wall leads to a drop down to a hands and knees crawl over crystal pools. After about 8m this ends at a calcited run-in which may dig.
   On November 1st, 2021, a hunting dog was rescued almost unharmed from the base of the shaft.
