Depth 2m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2012

A narrow shakehole has a 2m deep, too narrow rift at the south side and a 2m undescended drop at the northern end. To the east is an unexplored shakehole. The site was described as "pretty forlorn" at Easter 2012 and there was no draught at the possible dig.

Reference: anon., 2007b (Easter logbook); Corrin Juan, 2007a; anon., 2012b (Easter logbook)
