0132: cave
Mullir 30T 455287 4795442 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 697m
Length 53m Depth 11m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th October 2010

The original description stated that the "cave chokes after 15m", with a grid reference of VN55409565. The site was explored and surveyed in 2010, the entrance being described as a "big square hole" and called Chocolate Cave. A climb up at the base of the entrance slope was pushed through a small constriction to a small chamber with no way on.
A dig in a slot in the base of the chamber has a good echo with a Disto distance measured through of 14m. It needs a crowbar to progress but might be worthwhile.

References: Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981; pers comm.; anon., 2008c (Easter logbook); anon., 2010c (summer logbook); Corrin Juan, 2011
