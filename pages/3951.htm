3951: cave
Solórzano 30T 453615 4803676 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 185m
Length 5m Depth 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 14th October 2014

A large, deep depression, cleared of eucalyptus trees which are now (summer 2014) springing up again. At the base is a slide down into a choked, 5m long chamber. All apparent holes on the sides close in or are shadows, except for site 4044, a few metres higher and to the northeast of this cave.

Reference: anon., 2014b (Easter logbook); anon., 2014c (summer logbook)
