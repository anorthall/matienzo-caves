   A good, draughting underground dig in a wet weather stream sink. The site was enlarged in 1999 and the best way on may be down in the floor. Further work was carried out in the summer 2021 and on one session each in April and August 2022. Four sessions were worked in the summer 2025 with various opinions as to the quality of the dig.
   A bat was seen in the roof in December 2025.
