The information below has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club Dijon reference, Simonnot G, 2018.

   65 m south of the Cueva de las Campas (MCP4811 / SCD2400), at the foot of the same limestone corniche. A fissure, originally obstructed by blocks and earth topped with a pile of native stones. In rainy weather the sound of a water flow is particularly evident and motivated works. The conduit, however, appears to be small. Unlike the cueva de Campas no water outlet is visible here.
