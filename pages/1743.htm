Updated 3rd November 2002

The entrance lies 400m down the path from the farmhouses and about 30m up off the track where a small hole on the right in the track is a marker. Site 1821 with an arched cap is 20m further up the hill.
A 17m entrance pitch passes a sloping ledge to a boulder floor. This slopes down and enters a well decorated chamber. A hole in the wall at the boulder slope enters a crawl around the perimeter of the shaft which also enters the same chamber.
