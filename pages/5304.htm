   On the left bank and on the very edge of the río Asón, about fifty meters upstream from the Fuente LBT 191 (MCP5303 / SCD3417). At the foot of the limestone ledge, a small temporary water outlet has deepened the ground on the bank of the Asón. A small current of air comes out of a narrow duct cluttered with blocks. To unclog.
