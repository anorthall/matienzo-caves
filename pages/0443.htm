Updated 29th November 2012

The entrance is in a tree-lined depression and has a number of large blocks some of which may cover the draughting hole. A climb down through boulders to a draughting choke where stones drop for at least 10m, although this was found to be less when visited in the autumn, 2012. [The old grid reference is VN4977 9668; ETRS89: 30T 449668 4796471]
