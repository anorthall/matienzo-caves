Updated 16th November 2022

   The cave is at the top of the next field up above site 2113. It lies under a 1.5m high face and the entrance crawl leads to a stooping-sized chamber with bats. There are three possible ways on and a smoke test would be useful to show which route takes the inward draught.
    Guy Simonnot visited and "cleared" the hole on January 15, 2022, finding a "current of air then blowing at the entrance and at the back on the left".
