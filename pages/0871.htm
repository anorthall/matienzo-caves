Updated 26th April 2012; 27th January, 9th June 2025; 20th January 2026

   A tagged, choked, 3m deep shaft. The site was not found at Easter 2012.
When visited nearly 13 years later, it was described as a "3m deep shaft to a choked floor" with "a small hole in the floor and solid looking limestone". The drop was measured at 6m deep and seems bigger below. "This is a reasonable capping job and would make a grand dig."
   The site was dug on 2 occasions in December 2025.
