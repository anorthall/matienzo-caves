2587: Walled Cave
S Vega 30T 450133 4794702 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 495m
Length 28m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 10th June, 7th July 2007; 18th May 2023

A walled up bedding cave, about 50cm high. This was explored in late 2006. The end must be near the surface and if you did a bit of digging you could get a few extra metres. It was re-visited in April 2023.
