Updated 17th October, 5th November 2011; 2nd May, 28th September 2012; 5th December 2013

On the right of the track going uphill, an obvious entrance marked "AA76" in red paint. A small hole into a crawl which leads to a chamber with a slope up to the right and a slope down to the left. There is evidence of capping down on the left above a tight drop and signs that the cave might have been surveyed. The site could be associated with site 299 above.

The short tight drop has been descended to a small chamber with a possible dig in the floor.
