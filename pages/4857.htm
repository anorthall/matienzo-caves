4857: Moros, Cueva de los
Garzón 30T 450051 4804172 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 330m
Length 50m Depth 6m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   The west side of a large depression contains the large cave entrance. A short climb down enters a chamber about 30 x 20m wide. The cave has archaeological interest: human and animal bones have been found and there are black marks in small panels on the walls.

References: Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009 (survey); anon., 2019b (Easter logbook)
