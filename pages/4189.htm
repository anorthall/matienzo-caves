An excavated entrance enters a chamber with a rift passage dropping off to the left. At the northeastern end of the rift is a draughting dig. Lower jaw bones of piglet and sheep were discovered during the entrance digging.

In April 2026, the dig at the bottom was described as not having much draught and "didn't look promising".
