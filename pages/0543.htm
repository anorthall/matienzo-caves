Updated 19th February 1999; 17th September 2000; 25th March 2001; 16th February 2022

Re-discovered and partly re-explored during the 2000 summer expedition. The original, 1977, explorations found 6 shafts (20m, 21m, 23m, 24m, 23m, 23m) in a rift with false floors after a 5m entrance pitch. The survey in the 2000 logbook has 3 shafts in the floor with the rift continuing tight. The original exploration has been confirmed by an original explorer who "must have been much smaller in 1977!"
