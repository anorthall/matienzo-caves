2411: Dischuffed Chough Pot
Bosmartín 30T 449540 4797171 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 518m
Length 29m Depth 28m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 14th May 2011

A 20m pot that was not explored at Easter 2006 because of a nesting chough in the shaft. It was finally explored and surveyed at Easter 2011. A 15m pitch drops to a floor of bones and wire. A second pitch of 5m is found through a 4ft high arch. At the base is a slot in the floor and a third pitch of 7.4m. This has a mud floor with a miniscule stream disappearing in a 1" high groove.
