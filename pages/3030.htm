This is on the ledge above the base of the shakehole containing sites 3028 and 3029. There is a slight draught.
A description from 27/7/2016 states that the hole has a low entry into a 1m deep tightish rift between 15cm and 40cm wide, extending northwards behind the line of the cliff for 5m. A bulge at 3m prevents examination of the end.
