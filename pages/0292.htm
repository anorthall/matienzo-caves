0292: cave
Secadura 30T 455368 4799301 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 99m
Length 20m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 21st April 2013

Further up the depression from sites 291 and 293. A short climb down leads to a flat out crawl entering a mud filled chamber with no apparent exit. May repay another visit.
On a visit at Easter 2013, water flowing into the flatout crawl was diverted down another hole in the cave. The GPS taken at Easter 2013 is ETRS89: 30T 455360 4799292, probably putting it in the "wrong place".

References: anon., 1981a (logbook); Corrin J et al, 1981a; Corrin J, 1983c; anon., 2013b (Easter logbook)
