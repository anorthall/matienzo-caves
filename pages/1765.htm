Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 3rd November 2002

This site is described on the web site of the Sociedad Espeleológica Alto Duero who have explored it. It would appear that the entrance has been located with GPS but the altitude is wrong when plotted on 1:5000 maps. In the summer 2002, the labelled entrance was found some 200m away from the original grid reference (VN5517(7)9599(0) Alt 755m).

A 2m diameter entrance is the top of a 28m pitch. The bottom half of the pitch apparently gives a soaking.
