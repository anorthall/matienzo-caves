A 4m diameter shaft at the bottom of the depression. This bells out slightly and is about 30m deep landing in a large chamber with a slope to a second pitch. A spit was found at the top. The pitch is 17.5m but blind at the base. A horizontal passage above the second pitch goes for about 20m to some nice pretties.
