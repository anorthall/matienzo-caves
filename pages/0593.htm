0593: shaft
El Naso 30T 450468 4797621 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 502m
Length 15m Depth 13m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th May 2001; 27th November 2024; 17th May, 3rd October 2025

   A 3m climb down (ladder or free climb) in a rift to a floor of jammed boulders. There are two pitches descending further from this small bridge of boulders although the drop are probably the same pitch. First pitch is narrow at the top and will require capping, it's approx 10m deep. The 2nd pitch, approx 5m deep, requires some boulders removing to gain access, however, removal of the boulder floor you're standing on maybe an easier option. There appears to be a draught.
    A visit in April 2025 encountered a good strong outward draught all day. Plenty of mud and rocks were removed from a bridge which spans the shaft head. Just a couple of flakes remain which could be capped. The 7m shaft looks spacious below. [Pete O'Neill]
   The site was visited again in July 2025. The pitch head was opened up to squeeze through leading to a larger chamber below, about 4m x 6m. The pitch was 10m down to a rubble floor and the pitch-head is about 2m below the surface. There was no draught in the cave but a strong draught at Easter. The site deserves another look when there is a draught to find where there may be a way on down in the chamber. Larger explorers may like to enlarge the entrance into the pitch.
