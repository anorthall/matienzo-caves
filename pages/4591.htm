Length 1m Depth 1m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Above the Bosmartín-Calzadillas track and to the southeast of El Alto del Pozo is a substantial, vegetated depression with a small hole at the base and scattered cow bones. This is a possible dig with boulders visible 1m down. It may be worth putting in a couple of hours work, but there is no draught.
