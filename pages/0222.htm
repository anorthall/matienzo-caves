0222: Higuera, Torca de la
Fresnedo 30T 453048 4801171 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 146m
Length 10m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A 5m pitch used to land on boulders which slope down in a wide rift to a choke. In 1991 the pot was a climb down onto rubbish. A very tight hole at the base sometimes emits a strong draught.
