0967: shaft
El Naso 30T 451007 4796741 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 469m
Length 40m Depth 25m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 11th November 2000; 18th September 2013

A small scar contains the rift entrance - an 8m pitch into a chamber with a window above. A squeeze leads to a balcony above the 14m second pitch which ends at a too tight rift. A traverse over the top of the second pitch may reach another shaft. Part way down the entrance pitch is a step off into a blind 5m pot.
The site was partially re-explored in 2013 but needs gear.
