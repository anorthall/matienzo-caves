La Secada 30T 453072 4797849 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 272m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Hazel bushes surround a rift which was full of sandstone blocks. These have been excavated to a sandy mud fill. This is an easy site to dig and needs looking at in hot weather.
This site may have been the molephone "knocking point" in the Trident Passages in 1991.
