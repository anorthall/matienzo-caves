This site is on the side of a large depression. A large, interesting-looking shaft which needs vigilance when approached due to nearby open holes. The drop is about 15m with a ledge at one end about 5m down. At the bottom, a rift closes in and a crawl, just off the floor, ends at a sediment choke.

Reference: anon., 2008c (Easter logbook); anon., 2008e (summer logbook); Corrin Juan, 2009
