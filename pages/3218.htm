An excavated and stabilised shaft in the Piedrashitas (standing stones) area. One explorer had his foot squashed (luckily only bruised) during the inital excavations. The site lies close to the end of Gour Inlet in Cueva Hoyuca and may also provide a link to passages in Cueva Suviejo. Digging continues.
