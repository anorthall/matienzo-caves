5402: shaft
Cubija 30T 450262 4796726 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 303m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd May, 14th October 2025

    A "human sized" shaft with a chamber at the bottom. There is a spit anchor next to the hole.
    One ladder reaches a slightly sloping floor, 2 to 3m dia, with a continuing small passage turning to the left, looking in. A return visit is needed.
    The return in August 2025 explored the site. A small shaft is a couple meters wide and about 8m deep. The walls are calcited and on one side slippy. The bottom of the pitch consists of a mud and debris covered boulder/rubble heap to which a small tunnel can be seen to the side of the pitch. This tunnel is around 50cm by 50cm made of sharp knobbly rock and continues at a steeply sloping 45 degree angle for 4m-5m until a key hole leads into a small "chamber". This chamber has barely enough room to crouch and quickly ends in a pile of bones, rock and calcite. No draught present.
