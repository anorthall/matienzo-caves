An excavated 17m pitch which is covered with soft, white flowstone. Halfway down is a traverse to the east into a parallel blind aven. At the base, a small pool drains away through a narrow crack. On the opposite wall. a small rift drops 7 or 8m. This is currently too tight but an obstructing flake could be removed. There was a small outward draught with external temperatures at 14 - 16C.
