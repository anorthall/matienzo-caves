A 2m drop down a narrow rift that slopes to a bouldery chamber that has been dug. There is a vocal connection with nearby site 1867. It may be worth pursuing the dig (into a supposed continuation) as the point has been reached where the wall has turned under and the roof has anastomoses similar to those in site 1867.
