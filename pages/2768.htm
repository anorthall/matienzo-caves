Updated 15th April 2008; 26th April 2013

A shaft pointed out by the farmer from the opposite hillside. It is reputed to be a "deep shaft" situated just below a stand of eucalyptus and below the track. Despite a couple of searches, it was not found until Easter 2013 when it was shown to cavers by a local. The site turned out to be 12m deep.
