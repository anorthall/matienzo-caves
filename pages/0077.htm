*** Pre-Easter 2026 cave description ***


   Plotting the entrance on a modern, digital map (with the help of GPS) puts it at 350m altitude, 50m higher than the old maps.

    The 4 x 4m, strongly draughting entrance at the base of a cliff leads to a steep slope down to a 30m wide passage. Near the end of the second chamber, on the west side, a 7m pitch was spotted in August 2017. Just next to it, a climb down over calcite reaches a small chamber but there is no obvious way through to the base of the pitch. (Annotated survey). The pitch was descended at the end of March 2018 and a crawl followed back up to the main passage.
    The main passage route closes down at a couple of strongly draughting boulder chokes. The left hand side has been scaled. At Easter 1998, the left hand boulder choke was pushed through to a small passage and more boulders. The survey needs drawing up.
   Wolf Chamber is in this area: a climb over was aborted due to dodgy rock and a choke beyond the "wolf" skeleton has been dug to a chamber and an impossible choke. (6/8/03).
   The draughting choke to the right was excavated into a complicated section between moving boulders. This end of the cave was revisited in the summer, 2016, but "the boulder choke into the Easter '85 extension was too challenging for the cavers who had got through successfully over 30 years ago, ie cavers 30 years younger are needed to explore this extension which is still not fully explored. It's a shame that this cave with its massive chambers has been neglected and never finished." In August 2017, a squeeze at the initial chimney down into the choke (1985 extension) was capped out and about 20m of string laid following the original push.
    The draught here is very good: the lead was pushed in December 2017 and surveyed in a complex, strongly draughting area. The survey appears to overlap and diverge from the 1985 extensions, appearing to be a mainly "new" route. Although 104m was surveyed in batch 0077_17_01, the extra unexplored length is possibly about 80.7m and this is included in the length in the header. (Legs through to 0077_17_01.0.5 are flagged as duplicate).
   In August 2024, it was reported that, "The choke on the right (with the green string) was blowing a gale - got to be worthy of a bit more attention!"

  In April 2025, the right hand choke was passed after some effort, and a series of 8m high rifts entered (April 15th). At the second choke, a route was found up to the top of the boulders and a couple of large chambers to the head of a hading rift. The draught was very strong. The new extension was surveyed to station 61 at a pitch head. (Full description required!) The cave was completely resurveyed, giving a length 437m longer than before. The survey of the extension is linked below.
   Exploration continued over August 2025. On August 5th, the pitch was dropped but progress was not easy as explorers were "mired in various cul-de-sacs" or "spiralled downwards in a series of large draughty chambers". That day ended with 69m surveyed and half-a-dozen leads. (Batch 25-05). Some parts were named: Bridge Chamber, The Carousel, Carousel Chamber.
   Exploration continued on August 7th through a crawl and a descending slope into a "grotto of wonderful speleothems" called Lloyd's Grotto. Beyond, after a short climb down, a mud-floored section and pristine calcite, a "vast chamber" (Yahoo Chamber) was entered with "leads all over the place". One lead entered Maximum Breakdown Chamber and an "endless bedding plane". 274m were surveyed.
   On the same day, another team descended Bridge Chamber keeping straight on rather than going under the bridge. Resit Passage led to complicated junctions and Resit Chamber with a calcite desk and chair at the top of a short climb down into a nice gour chamber which closed down except for a possible high-level lead 10m up. Thirty metres further back, a draughting rift led to Pandemonium, a chamber 6m x 3m x 12m high with no less than 8 passable leads off it. The best looking lead: draughting and heading NW in the Mostajo direction was followed to another chamber with a similar number of ways off it. The ways downwards mostly close. The survey was tied into the other team's explorations.
   On August 10th, the Endless Bedding Plane was pushed over holes to a tall, draughting rift which requires 25m of rope. All side passages off Yahoo Chamber were also investigated. It was noted that the strong draught felt at the "calcite bridge" on August 5th also needed investigation.
   By the end of those 3 days of exploration, survey batches 25-05, -06, -07 and -08A totalled 663.1m, increasing the length of the cave to 1714m. A YouTube video shows some of these 2025 explorations.
   The squeeze in the first boulder choke was slightly widened and the dangerous cracked boulder wriggle, right at the start of the choke, was removed.
    It was noted that WhatsApp messages by 3G permeated more than 100m into the cave allowing a team to cancel a callout before they actually emerged. The first 30m of passage has a full 5G connection allowing live streams.
   The centre line survey can be seen below and the convoluted 2025 summer extensions have been drawn up.

2025 Summer Extensions - Passage descriptions (2025 - Pete Hall and Chris Scaife)

   At the end of Wind of Change, an 8m climb, left laddered in August 2025, leads to a choked rift with several ways closing down. At the far end of the rift on the left a way leads down to the left down a muddy descending tube that spirals down steeply and gradually enlarges. After 20m a junction is reached with a blind passage on the right and continuing slope down to the left into a chamber. This can also be reached by an alternative route from half way down the spiral. Right in the chamber leads to a traverse and climb. [Pete Hall]
   At the northern end of Shield Chamber, a crawl past stalactites and stalagmites leads into a spacious chamber. A climb up on the right here is unsurveyed, but is believed to reconnect with Shield Chamber. The way on is past fine formations in Lloyd's Grotto. Crawl carefully past long straws in this descending passage to a short climb down where care is required to avoid damage to the delicate speleothems. The crawl leads round to the right to enter the vast and complex Yahoo Chamber.
   There are numerous undescended holes in the floor in this chamber. Following the right wall around the edge of the chamber, there is a short loop on the opposite wall from the entry point to the chamber. Heading left from the entry point leads through several boulder piles to a slope upwards into Maximum Breakdown Chamber.
   Maximum Breakdown Chamber is wide and sloping, with a relatively low ceiling and much breakdown. It is above Pandemonium Chamber, which can be reached via a climb down. There are several undescended holes in the floor in this area. The top of the chamber leads into Endless Bedding Plane, a wide, low-ceilinged crawl towards a complex section that has not been fully investigated. A rope of at least 25m would be advised to descend into the rift at the end of the bedding plane. There seems to be a strong draught and the rift appears to be pointing towards Mostajo. [Chris Scaife]

   One male Stenasellus cf. virei Dollfus was collected in 1964 and 1 Greater Horseshoe bat recorded in April 2003.

   L. Mills found the Bronze age skeleton of a youth in the lowest part of the first chamber in 1975.  Both femurs were cut shortly before or after death. In 1999, small pieces of pottery were found in the same area.

   Further human remains were located in the central part of the chamber by C.A.E.A.P. in December 1989. These were subsequently removed: 124 pieces which were probably all from one 12 - 14 year-old. The remains of two hearths were also noted in the centre of the first chamber and a wall deposit seen in the entrance. The S.E.S.S. found pottery fragments, which they considered Iron Age, but which a revision might now class as medieval. A plan of the distribution of human bones recovered in 1999 is found here and full details of all the finds are to be found in Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Smith Peter et al, 2001. The age of the Bronze Age burial is given as 3999±59BP (radiocarbon years), approximately 2500BC (date calibrated years) (Smith P, Corrin J & Ruiz Cobo J, 2008).

   A low series of descending digs has been pursued (August 2025) at the lowest point of a low antechamber below the slide down from the entrance, but the route eventually closed in. Some parts are well decorated with "incredible antlers" formations. (See 2025 video; surveyed as part of batch 25-03 and 26-06)

    A tooth from a Giant European Cave Hyena (Crocuta spelaea) has been found. (Smith P, Corrin J and Ruiz Cobo J, 2008, p189).

   Photographs of some of the archaeological finds are pictured here.

   According to Quin (BU pp59-62), in his magnetic susceptibility studies, sediments from Rascavieja show similar k values to sediments in Cueva de Coberruyo (138), indicating that the sites may have had a common morphogenic agent and are connected.

    Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Smith Peter, 2003 has a useful summary of the archaeology, with photos and diagrams.

   Reference Smith P et al, 2015 has a table of the radio-carbon dates.

   Bats were seen flying near the entrance in the summer 2025.

*** Post-Easter 2026 cave description***

Chris Scaife, Pete Hall, George North and Becka Lawson
  The route through Choke 1, Choke 2, and Choke 3 (between Wind of Change and The Bridge) is now marked with reflective markers.

   The 4 x 4m, strongly draughting entrance at the base of a cliff leads to a steep slope down to a 30m wide passage. Keeping right here leads to a lower chamber with more bones where a dig in August 2025 dropped into smaller chambers which closed down after 10m.

   The entrance passage continues in grand style up and down slopes through the first chamber with daylight visible for a considerable distance. A steep slope leads up through an arch and drops down again into the second chamber. Here, an oxbow doubles back but a climb between loose boulders has not been attempted to rejoin the first chamber. A crawl over flowstone leads to a beautiful helictite chamber (unsurveyed.) More slopes soon lead to a complex junction which is easily missed at the Bone Cairn. Descending massive boulders on the right hand side of the main passage leads down to a mud floored passage with a fine sculpted roof. At the end of this passage a drafting hole up on the right hand side leads through an unstable boulder choke into an ascending rift passage into Wolf Chamber. This is 40 x 20 x 10m high, boulder floored breakdown chamber with a wolf skeleton on the far side.

    Back at the Bone Cairn, the right-hand passage leads down a slope past some distinctive purple deposits. The way into Choke 1 is a passage on the left at the base of the slope. A strongly drafting 2.5m deep shaft with an awkward manoeuvre leads into a more open area. In 2026 fraying green string and reflectors guides the way through the choke. A zig-zag leads almost back to the foot of the shaft before heading onwards again through more bouldery crawls and up a 3m chimney. Soon a squeeze through a horizontal slot is reached (enlarged to comfortable dimensions in 2026.) A further vertical rift leads to a 2.5m rift up into a small solid chamber marking the end of Choke 1 and of the other end of the green string.

   From here, a slope down leads to a short walk through a meandering rift and reunites with an alternative high level route after 15m. A meandering route following cairns generally keeps to the left-hand side, while ramps between blocks can be seen heading up to the right. After about 50m the correct ramp is reached and is followed up short steps to the start of Choke 2. This starts feet first down through a low gravelly slot. Beyond this, further slots up, down and right are tight but straight on leads the feet down a short drop into a small chamber between blocks. Beyond, an easy squeeze over a slab leads to an area of rifts with boulder obstacles passing a couple of corners to an obvious dig. On the right here, a blind series with 50m of unsurveyed passage has been partly blocked with digging spoil. A 1m drop down an enlarged rift leads into a flat out crawl for 5m to a T-junction. Left closes down immediately but right soon pops up over a slab into walking sized passage. Straight on at this level closes down after 20m (unsurveyed) but a draughting 2.5m climb leads into a fractured passage with marshmallow formations that soon leads over a pot and pops out into a 6m x 6m tunnel, Wind of Change. This starts as easy walking before a couple of boulder obstacles and a climb up a steep ramp leads to a large chamber where the passage divides. The larger, left-hand passage closes down in breakdown whilst the right hand branch leads through two more chambers to station 61. Between the two passages, a large block about 15x10 x10m has subsided leaving a fracture plane that can be followed via a cross-over passage into a Continuation of the Wind of Change leading through two chambers to a draughting choke.

   At the end of Wind of Change, an 8m climb, (best rigged with a ladder from a natural but derigged after Easter 2026), leads to a choked rift with several ways closing down. The way on from here was marked with reflectors in 2026. At the far end of the rift on the left a way leads down to the left and descends a muddy descending tube that spirals down steeply and gradually enlarges. After 20m a junction is reached with a blind passage on the right and continuing slope down to the left into a chamber. This can also be reached by an alternative route from halfway down the spiral. Right in the chamber leads to a traverse and 3m climb down to a junction at Station 77. Left gets too narrow after 5m. On the right is a draughting 3m climb into a narrow walking passage that soon leads to Bridge Hall with an exposed rock bridge leading across to the western side past 8m drops on both sides. Beyond here, the remainder of the cave is a complex 3D maze that defies a full and clear description but here is an attempt.

   After the rock bridge the ways divide with an 8m climb down to the bottom of the chamber (and hence on to Resit Chamber or continuing down to Below Bridge Hall) or a traverse forward leading to a short passage to Shield Chamber.

   Shield Chamber starts with a shield formation and a pitch down into a lower chamber. This can be bypassed by keeping right and following the right wall to a junction with several ways:
     • Straight ahead leads by clambering to Upper Lloyd's Grotto and back down to the spacious chamber before Lloyd's Grotto
     • Right down a hole in the floor is an undescended 15m pitch with a vocal connection to the Balcony at the head of the Squirrel Sanctuary. This can also be reached from the undescended trench in Lower Shield Chamber
     • To the left, a 5m traverse on large flowstone holds on the right wall leads to a flowstone bridge. From here a further traverse can be bypassed by a 3m climb up the left wall and down through a wall rift. Here another 15m of walking passage closes down.
     • Also to the left under the bridge leads into Lower Shield Chamber decorated with flowstone. A step across a floor rift here reaches a side chamber with a gour pool. Down the trench is a 15m undescended trench with a vocal connection to the Balcony at the head of the Squirrel Sanctuary

   At the northern end of Lower Shield Chamber, a crawl past stalactites and stalagmites leads into a spacious chamber. A climb up on the right here leads to Upper Lloyd's Grotto with more fine formations before reconnecting with Shield Chamber. The way on is past fine formations in Lloyd's Grotto. Crawl carefully past long straws in this descending passage to a short climb down where care is needed to avoid damage to the delicate speleothems. The crawl leads round to the right to enter the vast and complex Yahoo Chamber. There are numerous undescended holes in the floor in Yahoo Chamber. Following the right wall around the edge of the chamber, there is a short loop on the opposite wall from the entry point to the chamber. Heading left from the entry point leads through several boulder piles to a slope upwards into Maximum Breakdown Chamber.
   
   Maximum Breakdown Chamber is wide and sloping, with a relatively low ceiling and much breakdown. It is above Pandemonium Chamber, which can be reached via a climb down. There are several undescended holes in the floor in this area. The top of the chamber leads into the Endless Bedding Plane, a wide, low-ceilinged crawl ending at a long, deep cross rift with numerous ways down. At the eastern end is a 10m pitch called Overconsumption which enters a complex of unsurveyed passages. To the east of the base of the pitch a window leads through to a crater. A traverse around the crater leads to a hading rift with an 8m pitch down at the western end – Underconsumption – that drops down to a mud choked floor with no draft. Passages leading east from Overconsumption would benefit from a further inspection.

   From the base of the climb down at Bridge Hall, go northwest to Resit Passage which is easy going through a fine flowstone chamber where a short passage on the left ends in a small chamber. The passage ends at another flowstone chamber, Resit Chamber.

   30m back from Resit Chamber a narrow way on the outside of a bend in the passage leads after about 10m to a climb down with a distinctive pillar at the top into Pandemonium Chamber. Although it is a little more awkward, the Resit Passage route is recommended to reach Maximum Breakdown Chamber in order to bypass the vulnerable formations in Lloyd's Grotto.

   Pandemonium Chamber has about 8 ways on visible in all directions, some more enticing than others. A low lead to the north leads through a short crawl into another small chamber Everything Everywhere All at Once, before heading northwest through a series of small chambers before emerging in a 15 x 8m chamber, The Multiverse, where a boulder slope leads down into breakdown. On the right, a long overhang looks unclimbable but a dripping chimney gives easy access to the west wing of the chamber where the undescended 20m pitch and other holes in the Endless Bedding Plane enter from the ceiling. At the top of The Multiverse, the Inclined Bedding Plane enters, making an unpleasant round trip back to Pandemonium Chamber.

   A sloping ledge in Pandemonium Chamber has two ways on:
     • A bold step leading to a the bottom of Maximum Breakdown Chamber.
     • A climb over a slab leads to the passage between Yahoo Chamber and Maximum Breakdown Chamber

   Back once again at the bottom of Bridge Hall, an obvious window on the left, Under the Bridge, starts with a short climb down to where a crawl leads off above the blind pit below. The crawl leads immediately to a further 7m climb down a distinctive tube like a calcite pillow throat. This ends at a 2m climb/squeeze over calcite that drops in at the south end of a chamber (immediately at the base of the squeeze a low right connects in two places to Scampi Lardos). A low hole on the right wall of the chamber opens onto a 2.5m drop that is the easiest route into Scampi Lardos.

   On the left wall of the chamber a short climb down leads to a complex area, A Flock of Squirrels. Going through a tight slot or crawling under immediately reaches a right that leads, via a climb through a hole into Scampi Lardos where the climb from the chamber enters. Going ahead instead, the next right quickly becomes too tight. There is then a 4-way junction. Right soon leads down a climb to a complex, unsurveyed, boulder area; ahead soon ends at a high aven; left climbs up through a narrowing to reach Dead Pony Chamber, a section of large bouldery passage closed at both ends with no way on.

   Back at the entry point into Scampi Lardos, ascending to the right leads to calcited grottos with holes in the roof that are assumed to lead to undescended holes down in the Lloyd's Grotto area. From a balcony, a 5m pitch (undescended because it can be bypassed) leads to a larger decorated passage connected to The Squirrel Sanctuary. Going instead to the left, around a large fallen slab, leads to a short crawl and 2m climb down to enter The Squirrel Sanctuary. Up slope to the right leads to the base of the 5m pitch from the balcony.

   Left at the Squirrel Sanctuary leads around a short calcite traverse to a draughting sand-floored crawl. 25m of flat-out crawl leads to much larger passage. A passage to the right leads to mud pools but has not been pushed. A slope down straight ahead leads to fine formations and a roof inlet (Beauty and the Beast). A traverse around the right side of the pit here leads to a calcited rift with two ways on:
     • A climb down to floor level leads to a low, wet, muddy crawl and a squeeze to a small calcite chamber (The Abomination) with an undescended and promising draughting 8m pitch in the floor, which appears to drop into a larger rift passage.
     • A step over a rift leads to a sandy crawl past formations to a junction with a larger passage. Right leads to an undescended rift climb down (a good lead in a good place). Left leads over fallen boulders to a larger chamber, The Quantum Realm, where an aven is directly beneath Pandemonium Chamber.

   The pit below the aven is blind. A draughting rift down on the right side of the passage and further sharp rift leads steeply down to the final chamber, 15m vertically below the The Quantum Realm. This is the lowest point of the cave, but there appears to be no passable way on here.
