1506: Road Show, The
La Secada 30T 451734 4797665 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 244m
Length 46m Depth 12m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 11th February 2001; 2nd May 2024

   A body-sized entrance in the rock outcrop 2m above the road. A squeeze through leads to a decorated remnant that chokes after 15m. The tight rift down below the entrance enters a small breakdown chamber under the road and descends over more blocks to a sand / mud floored fossil passage that chokes after 20m. There are possible digs at both ends.
    The end of the high level passage was excavated for an hour or so in April 2024 and more work is required. There appeared to be no obvious way on in the lower level.
