0354: cave
El Naso 30T 451955 4796251 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 355m
Length 100m Depth 26m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 17th October 2003; 19th November 2007; 27th January 2008; 20th June, 10th September 2021

   A small, vertical slot entrance to a slope leads to a chamber. Further inclines on calcite lead to a mud choke. The cave contains some good formations.
   The 2021 resurveyor found the bottom half of the cave "more confusing and awkward" than the initial survey 40 years ago. A small addition has been made to the surveyed length to take account of the passage not entered at the final drop.
