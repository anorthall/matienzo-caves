A vegetated depression, downhill and to the east of site 0306. A slither down the entrance slope leads to the top of a 7m pitch which chokes with mud. A parallel shaft, entered from a climb up from the bottom, has not been descended; there may be possiblities in a rift at the far side.

At the start of the Easter 2006 activities, the site was the scene of a successful calf rescue.
