2801: resurgence
El Camino 30T 452637 4796929 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 149m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2012; 18th May 2023; 13th October 2025; 22nd May 2026

   A small, built over resurgence on the west side of the track. There are boulders in the field opposite which may mark the route of the culverted stream. No sound of water could be heard along here in a dry April 2023.

   Kick sampling at the end of July 2025 (Hollie London) revealed shrimp with pigment and, speculatively, with behavioural and physical similarities to albino shrimp found in Riaño. Many juvenile fire salamanders and Gammarus sp were seen in late March 2026.
