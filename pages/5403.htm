5403: Chamber Pot
Cubija 30T 450284 4796682 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 300m
Length 25m Depth 20m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 23rd May, 14th October 2025

    A dig which leads into a flat out crawl where stones are heard to bounce down a slope for one or two seconds.

   The site was opened up at Easter 2025. The first drop (p6) is to a large ledge. From here, the second pitch (p10) reaches the floor of a large chamber with stal. The apparent two ways on are both alcoves. There may be other possibilities in the floor but there was no noticeable draught when explored.
   A cursory look to the 1st pitch head was made in July 2025.

Rigging notes : pitches are approx p6 + p10, requiring ca 30m rope and 5 bolts. All bolts are thru-bolts and need anchors. First pitch starts from a bolt on surface to a second bolt at the far end of the entrance slot (tricky to rig), then drop 3m or so to a ledge where there is a third bolt for a deviation (placed so could also be used as a rebelay). Drop to the floor, then continue to a Y-hang (two more bolts) from opposite walls for the final 10m drop into the chamber.
