4772: Sima Grande de Las Calzadillas, La
Las Calzadillas 30T 447404 4795092 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 492m
Length 98m Depth 39m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 27th May 2019; 10th February 2020

   A p25 in a 20m long shaft chokes.

   Further exploration was carried out in the autumn of 2018 when a chimney in one corner was descended to a parallel shaft that was opened up after several hours work then descended 4m to a draughting constriction. Work continued in May and June 2019 when the constriction was eventually opened up allowing a p20 to de dropped to a small chamber with the draught coming from a rock-choked rift under one wall.
