2753: cave
Seldesuto 30T 448534 4794527 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 390m
Length 50m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 27th September 2012; 12th October 2025

   A 12m passage ends at an excavated mud section where a further dig in calcite can be seen with a possible passage beyond. There is a good draught. (There may be a problem with the grid reference due to tree coverage). A constriction (passed in 2010) was removed in 2012 and the small cave completely (re) explored and surveyed. The draughting 10-20cm wide slot at the end will need enlarging for at least 4m.
   In 2025, the end (and the entrance) was enlarged and access gained to a small chamber. There is a draughting continuation. The passage turns sharply right and further passage can be seen for another few meters but needs a calcite blockage enlarging to make further progress. The way on is still small. The total length of new accessible passage is about 6m (not surveyed).
