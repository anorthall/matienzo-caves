5362: Tree Shroom Hole
La Secada 30T 453032 4798514 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 270m
Length 11m Depth 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th, 26th, 31st January 2024

    An open shaft with the remains of a 3-pronged tree trunk a couple of metres down. Below, there are 2 narrow rifts that appear to be about 4.5 and 3.5m deep.
On New Year's Day 2024, one rift was dropped for 4m but the parallel shaft needs enlarging to reach the base.
