2227: Fridge Hole
La Gatuna 30T 449555 4799229 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 143m
Depth 2m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

A built-over hole at the base of a steep sided depression. A stone fridge has a wooden door and grilles over a tiny 2m deep hole that draughts. A video camera dropped down the hole has shown a possible rift. A return is required on a hot day to see if any draught emerges from behind the fridge.
