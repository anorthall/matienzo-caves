Length 50m Depth 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 27th April 2012; 18th October 2015

An open, draughting cave in a small cliff face. One tube at the entrance is 10m long and offers some climbing but the main route is at lower level past excavated sections to a 4m pitch into a chamber with fridge-sized boulders. In September 2015, visitors were unable to find a safe belay for the p4 and so were unable to check the final choke.
