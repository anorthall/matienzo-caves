0869: cave
La Secada 30T 453069 4798272 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 243m
Length 18m Depth 6m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 5th May 2001; 26th April 2012; 22nd May 2017; 27th January 2025

   An open 5m x 1.5m rift which has a 3m climb to a descending mud floor to a choke. A possible dig but no draught. Tagged 869.
   The site was revisited at Easter 2012 and described as "a big rift with an obvious mature tree at one end. A handline is required "to get out again" and the rift shows signs of solution.
   The pre-GPS grid reference was VN53179848 Alt. 245m; ETRS89: 30T 453068 4798271; a 2012 GPS put the hole at 30T 453068 4798271; the 2017 GPS position is shown above.
   The site was revisited in 2025 for photos and video.
