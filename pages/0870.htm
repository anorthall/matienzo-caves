Length 12m Depth 10m
Area posiion : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 26th April 2012; 22nd May 2017; 27th January, 9th June 2025

   A choked 10m pitch which is tagged 870.
   At Easter 2012, the site was described as "an 8m shaft with saplings".
   The pre-GPS grid reference was VN53199848 Alt. 257m; first GPS reading was 30T 453085 4798263 and the Easter 2017 reading is shown above.
   When revisited during the winter 7 years later, it was described as being packed with bones, rusty tin cans of varying sizes, many broken bottles, some shoes and an old enamelled pan. This rubbish could be a midden / household rubbish dump for a nearby occupied cave / cabaña and may be classed as an archaeological site, depending on the depth / age of the rubbish.
