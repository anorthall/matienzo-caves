A rock shelter, with decayed formations, is 10m front to back and 6m wide. A slope to the right drops 5m to the lowest, draughtless point. Tagged.

To the north are 4 shakeholes in a line, all about 3 - 5m deep, again with no draught. A couple of them are pictured here. A couple were teased out as sites 5399 and 5400 in January 2024.
