2903: Church Entrance (Hoyuca entrance)
Riaño 30T 451599 4799753 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 175m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2012; 2nd November 2015; 1st July 2018; 16th November 2025

The Church Entrance to Cueva de la Hoyuca (site 107), so called as it can be easily accessed down the field to the south of Riaño church. A climb down just inside the entrance meets a crawl that emerges in Quadraphenia. In the 1990's, one section of passage was inundated with cow shit which, by October 2015, had completely disappeared.
