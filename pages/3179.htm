Length 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 30th October 2010

A sandstone outcrop with undercut which has remnants of some form of 'industry'. A small drystone wall with opening into an area approx 3m x 5m containing two long log trestle tables and a tea chest. In the far right of the undercut is a small drystone wall past which the undercut continues further under the outcrop for approx 5m before swinging left and closing up completely. No signs of draught on a hot day. Old animal bones on floor. No dig potential.
