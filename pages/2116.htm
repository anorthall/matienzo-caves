Length 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th November 2004

This cave, high above the track running past Torca de Azpilicueta, is an old, choked cave with roof pendants. The entrance is about 8m wide and low at the back with a number of possible digs. It may be worthwhile digging, as the entrance gives the impression of a large passage choked with sediment.
The digs on the right and centre of the cave appear to draught.
