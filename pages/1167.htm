1167: Hondo, Torca El

Length 136m Depth 57m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 19th February 1999; 16th April 2008

An impressive shaft which is best tackled on ladders. The 30m entrance pitch drops onto a large boulder slope which bends round into a breakdown area which appears to be quite unstable. The pot appears to have been previously surveyed (by Spaniards). An excellent spot with a fine view back up the slope to the entrance pitch with daylight flooding in.
