Length 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 14th October 2014; 23rd May, 5th October 2015

This hole is about 20m from and level with passages off the Big Chamber Somewhere Near the Entrance in Cueva de Carcavuezo. The site was located by a surface survey from Carcavuezo entrance. Digging in the summer 2014 appeared to reach a back wall but there is still a cool draught. Further digging the following Easter suggested that excavating to the right may be under a detached block.
