5529: Skull and Crossbones dig
El Naso 30T 451301 4797150 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 309m
Length 1m Depth 1m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   This dig has an arched roof with cow bones and rubbish. The excavated entrance lies on the North side of the shake hole. Large boulders have been removed with a 1 - 1.5m hole revealed with sloping roof at 60° consisting of mud and cobbles with some signs of solid limestone. Holes in the floor are draughting. An exposed rift was widened and a trench from the entrance started to increase vertical space for further removal of sand and cobbles.

Reference: anon., 2026a (Easter logbook)
