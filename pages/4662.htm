Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 19th November 2022

The entrance lies in the shakehole below Cueva de Fuente de la Pila (0486) and is about 10m long, with side passages on either side. It must be related to the small passages heading northeast from the main tunnel in 0486. It has been surveyed by Asociación Lobetum of Cuenca.
