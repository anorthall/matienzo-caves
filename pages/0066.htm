An elliptical shaft with a hazel tree. A 23m deep, 15m diameter shaft leads to a boulder slope at the base of which is a small hole and the second pitch of 18m. A boulder floor slopes down to a possible dig. "Upstream" quickly chokes.

Marked 596 with orange tape.
