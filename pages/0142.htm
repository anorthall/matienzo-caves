Length 22m Depth 22m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 9th November 2003

A short squeeze down into a draughting entrance leads to the head of a pitch. The belay is 2m down a tight rift and the pitch is 19m deep, landing on a roomy ledge. A 3m climb down enters an enlargement that chokes in both directions, the draught being lost.

References: anon., 1980a (logbook) (survey); Corrin J et al, 1981b (survey); Corrin J S and Smith P, 1981
