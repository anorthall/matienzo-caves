0092: Rocabado, Sima de
La Secada 30T 452400 4798028 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 257m
Length 76m Depth 76m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 21st May, 1st June 2014

The entrance pitch of 8m is in a shakehole. A climb and crawl up through a window on the right leads to the head of the second pitch. This is 70m deep and is choked at the bottom with silt and cobbles. There is a strong inwards draught that is lost on the descent. Windows seen on the descent are also blind. The bottom of the pot appears to be close to the Haymarket Extensions in the Western Series of Cueva de Carcavuezo (0081).
