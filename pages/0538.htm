0538: cave
El Naso 30T 450648 4796891 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 462m
Length 15m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance is in a small depression 10m from the field boundary. A 1x1m hole leads into a flat-roofed rock shelter. A wall has been built along the back wall and there is a narrow slot in the floor to a lower chamber with a calcite ramp. From here there is a choked, draughtless crawl.

References: anon., 1985b (logbook); anon., 1992a (Easter logbook)
