Length 36m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2002; 14th September 2021

The stooping height entrance is just off the GR75 path, in a shallow gulley, and leads to a 36m long chamber that bends round to the left. Small, calcited pits in the floor may have been dug for watering goats.
