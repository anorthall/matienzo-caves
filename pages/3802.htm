Cubija 30T 450109 4796810 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 271m
Length 4m Depth 3m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 10th October 2013; 4th October 2017

A depression quite close to the western end of the main passage in Torca de Mostajo. The dig is boulders in a descending tube and looks a reasonable prospect. Excavation took place on a couple of occasions in July 2013 where a small enlargement could be seen at the bottom to the south. Shoring and much more work is required.
A visit in August 2017 was unable to reach the site due to it being very overgrown.

Reference: anon., 2013b (Easter logbook); anon., 2013d (summer logbook); anon., 2017c (summer logbook)
