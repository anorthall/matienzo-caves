5495: Oscura, Cueva
Enaso 30T 451934 4796460 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 370m
Length 20m Depth 4m
Area position: Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   The easiest route to this cave would appear to be walking out along El Nanso then dropping down to the entrance.
   The entrance, obscured by long grass and gorse, lies at the base of a sandstone cap. The GPS coordinates above are accurate. Although only short, the cave is full of stal which create three routes in. Straight ahead is a crawl down to where it is possible to stand. To the right, a narrow route is partly choked with stal but doesn't appear promising. A slide down to the left between stal reaches a wall and an unexplored, narrow 3m drop in calcite to standing water. This appears to have no outlet. Continuing left, a short route ascends to choke.
    The cave has not been surveyed but appears to be about 20m long.
