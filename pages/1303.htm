1303: resurgence
N Vega 30T 450718 4795861 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 169m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

This site is to the north of the road, halfway between the La Vega church and the next house to the west. A wet weather resurgence where water bubbles up through grass next to a cabbage patch and water can be seen falling in a rift some 5m behind against the hillside. Needs investigating.
