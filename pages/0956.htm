Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 18th January 2004; 23rd January, 21st May 2026

Shown in Actividades Regionales. Exploraciones en Cantabria (anon., 1993a) as site 20. Approximate grid reference taken from the map in the same booklet is 30T 456098 4793541 which is wrong. The above GPS reading is for the N entrance. The southern entrance is in a large depression with site 5515 up the eastern slope..

From the northern entrance, keeping left and crawling under leads to a short step up and a 5m pitch down to sand/mud fill which has been interpreted as a rising. Keeping right after the entrance has walking passage to the southern entrance. Bats were seen in the cave.
