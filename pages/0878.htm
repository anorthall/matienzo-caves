The entrance lies some 20m below La Cuvia (086). A handline climb of about 5m leads to a chamber with numerous cow bones. A draughting stal blockage was opened up to reveal the skeleton of a dog; beyond is a draughting choke which has been dug. The site contains a possible hearth and claw scratchings on stal.
