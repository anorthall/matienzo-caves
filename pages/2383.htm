In a hillock above Famous Five Cave (Cueva del Bocarón). A walk-in entrance to a 10m diameter chamber with no way on. Flints and ceramics have been found. (See Ruiz Cobo J and Muñoz Fernández E, 2013, p368)

Reference: anon., 2006b (Easter logbook); Ruiz Cobo J and Muñoz Fernández E, 2013
