The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

   A little to the east (c200m?) and below the large Cueva de la Rasa (MCP5135 / SCD3148). Beautiful porch and cave of about ten meters ending on a totally impenetrable squeeze (no draught). To the left of the entrance a low and wide lateral tube, occupied by badgers, emerges through a small entrance in the cornice.
