1333: Tortilla, Torca
S Vega 30T 450209 4793985 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 613m
Length 35m Depth 28m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 30th April 2005

A gash in rock leads to a climb down into a steeply descending rift passage. After a stal slope, a very tight pitch descends to a mud choke. A pitch on the right at the foot of the stal slope has a sound connection to the first pitch.

The good draught out is lost on the way in.
