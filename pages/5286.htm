The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The altitude is taken off the MCP QGIS map.

     In the southern part of the Garma de Piqueras. To get there you have to climb from Bustablado in the direction of Cullaherboso and Garma Blanca passing through Tabladillo.
   A 5 x 4m sinkhole with vertical walls. A 4 m drop leaves us at the bottom of this doline where a narrow meander gives access to a 4 m pitch. The continuation is blocked by stones; the draught is completely absent.
