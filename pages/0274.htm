Length 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 9th October 2004; 10th December 2006

A strongly draughting bedding entrance with boulders. At the back of the cave is an aven with a strong draught. In 2006 it was noticed that the main way on appears to be a bedding on the left (up a step or climb in from the right). This needs more digging to follow a good, cold draught.
