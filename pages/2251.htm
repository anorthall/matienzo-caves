A draughting site with a tinned roof at the north end of a grassy depression. Two blocks at the entrance have been removed to allow entry into a 8.5m by 4m chamber, 1.5 high. There are lots of cow heads on floor - it is clear the south wall was open and used as a dump! A dig on the south side has a small draught out but will need a major excavation due to loose fill.
