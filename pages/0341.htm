A hole with a "continuing slope" which was finally investigated in 2003. The cave links down a tight slope with the high level passage in Torca del Serruco (site 50). Large fragments of prehistoric pottery have been found in Torca del Serruco where they would have rolled in from site 341.
See site 50 for pictures and video.
