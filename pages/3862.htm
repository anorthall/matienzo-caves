3862: Felipé, Cueva de
Hornedo 30T 448109 4801225 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 126m
Length 8m Depth 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 12th May 2018

   A 3 x 2m cave entrance in the eucalyptus trees. A cold, mossy entrance leads to an undescended 4m rift on the right which may continue. It could be associated with the passages heading north to sandstone chokes in Torca la Vaca.
   On a visit in April 2018, the cave was too tight along the rift and it was thought the cave was on the verge of collapsing.
