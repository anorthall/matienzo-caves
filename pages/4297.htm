Updated 25th September 2018; 17th November 2022

The information below has been roughly translated from the French, Spéléo-Club Dijon reference, Simonnot G, 2016.

At the foot of a rocky ledge, at the high limit of a meadow. To the left of the entrance a small route closes after 3 m. On the right we can go on all fours to a 4 or 5 m gallery that could be extended. There is no draught.
Explored by SC Dijon and Chablis SC, July 2007
