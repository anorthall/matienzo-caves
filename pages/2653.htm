This site is about 10m from the eastern edge of the resurgence pool at Los Boyones. A slot down into a space - one way seems to drop to water, the other at a higher level continues open out of sight.
This was "possibly explored" in May 2010 (account near end of summer logbook). A squeeze down into passage, about 5m long and choked.

Reference: anon., 2007b (Easter logbook); Corrin Juan, 2007a; anon., 2010c (summer logbook)
