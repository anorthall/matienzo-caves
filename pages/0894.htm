A wine store with an audible draught next to the track. A visit in 2009 encountered a very good draught and the site was excavated over a couple of occasions in 2010. The site was "made good" and covered with a wall when the farmer came across the digging mess. There was no sign of an imminent breakthrough.
Other digs in the "Mess" area were also investigated. See sites 3460, 3461, 3462, 3463.
