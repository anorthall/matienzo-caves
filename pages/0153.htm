0153: Tree Root Cave
La Gatuna 30T 449528 4799781 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 133m
Length 80m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 4th October 2011

A tight, flat-out slot above a choked stream sink enters a well decorated chamber with a number of short passages and holes in the floor. All choke or become too tight.
While dowsing to the south of entrance in 2011, the farmer in the nearby house was most unfriendly and insisted that visitors on his land were not welcome.

References: anon., 1982 (logbook); Corrin J, 1983c; anon., 2011d (summer logbook)
