Length 70m Depth 44m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 11th October 2013

A site in rough pasture. A 9m ladder descent off trees ends on a boulder slope of jammed blocks. Further exploration occurred in August 2013 when a hole in the NE corner of the floor was descended down a large, 22m shaft on rope, past jammed blocks and several rebelays, to a sloping floor of debris and blocks. This point is about 40m below surface and there is no way on at the base of the final boulder slope without some serious digging.
