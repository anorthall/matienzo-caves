Ideopuerta 30T 446811 4796235 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 358m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th April 2012; 19th September 2013; 18th April 2016; 7th May 2024

A man-made entrance to a soft, moist sandy floor and a good draught. Probably used as a fridge.

The strongly draughting site was excavated at Easter 2012 to a narrow slot to where snappers would be needed to progress. It was found draughting in November 2015 and continued to look interesting - requiring a serious digging effort - in April 2024.
