5080: shaft
La Secada 30T 453249 4798450 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 287m
Length 5m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 17th November 2025

  Discovered while searching for site 0229. A shaft hidden by a heather bush at the base of a small limestone outcrop. At 5m depth, a 3 inch wide rift continues. There is no draught.
  After site 0229 was rediscovered in November 2025, it became clear that site 5080 was actually 0249. Sites 0229 and 0249 had been found on the same day in 1981 with both being placed too high on the map.
  The description for 0249 reads, "A hole under a blackberry bush drops into a small chamber. The 1981 BCRA Transactions has 249 and 250 at Beralta - numbers which apparently duplicated #0177 and #0178. The site was "relocated" in 1981: 1981 logbook, p6 has the "relocation".

   Because code 5080 has a number of resources attached, site 5080 will be kept and site 0249 eventually reallocated.
