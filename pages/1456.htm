Length 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 30th October 2020; 21st September 2023

The entrance lies about 50m down the track from site 1455 and is again a very short walk to the entrance. This is a circular tube about 2m above the ground. The passage continues for about 10m before closing in.
The site may have some archaeological interest. In October, 2020, the area around the site had been cleared revealing other small holes. The site has been repositioned from 30T 450168 4796711. A visit in August 2023 concluded that there was no digging potential as the site was totally choked with calcite.
