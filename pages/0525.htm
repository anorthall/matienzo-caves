0525: Gato Montés, Cueva del
Cubija 30T 450468 4796271 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 230m
Length 20m Depth 14m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 13th May 2023; 2nd October, 14th November 2025; 15th May 2026

   A draughting hole drops down 3m to an 8m crawl to a diggable choke. A tight rift passage heads into the hillside from the entrance chamber, filled with mud. It is possible to squeeze 3m before it becomes too tight with some draught. In April 2023, the positioning was found to be OK and it was thought that the site might be suitable for a "Wednesday Dig".
   The site was re-investigated down to metal work and old cable then a draughting rift at the top of the hole (guarded by a metal scaffolding plank) was opened up and descended to the top of a draughting p11. However, further work caused the collapse of the hole over the top of the p11 and the pitch remains undescended. It is likely to need shoring to allow access to the pitch.
   Further visits in October 2025 saw the site being surveyed and some stabilising carried out. A wildcat (Felis silvestris) was seen at the cave entrance with cat droppings and small bones observed in the cave.
   By April 2026, the roof had fallen in and a large boulder had also slipped out the wall. The site has been left as "too big a job".
