   A walking-size passage to a complete choke after 12m.

    The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

    In the wooded ledge (maquis) just above the Arredondo cemetery. Access, however, must be via the Llaneces track and the overlying meadow.
    The cave comes down to a beautiful rectilinear fossil gallery of 12 m. The section shows side benches and the walls are everywhere marked by the work of the water.
