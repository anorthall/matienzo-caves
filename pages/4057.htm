   A rift parallel to and above a new road through a eucalyptus plantation. The site has three points of entry described below from west to east.
1: A 3m climb down into a cross rift. All become too tight in less than 2m. A hole in the floor chokes but has a draught.
2: A 3m descent into the rift which is completely choked at both ends.
3: A tight rift chokes after 4m.
