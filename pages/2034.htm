Length 22m Depth 17m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th November 2004; 11th February 2016; 24th September 2018; 16th November 2022

A 1.5m diameter, 11m deep shaft where the obvious bushes are below the track. At the base, the passage drops round to the right and becomes very narrow with a skeleton on a mud floor beyond. This may have been descended by the SCD in 2010 where "many goat and cow bones litter the ground at the end which has a small aven. No draught."
