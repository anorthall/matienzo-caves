Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 10th October 2022; 24th January, 9th June 2025

   A "nice", straight pitch to a possible draughting dig. Although, according to the first documented visit in 1977, there was no draught!
   The shaft was checked out 47 years later when a narrow rift to one side, half way down, was noted. It appears to be bigger beyond the constriction but would require capping to open up. More documentation was also produced: survey, photos and video.
