0975: Cubija, Abrigo de la (Marcos, Cueva de)
Cubija 30T 450383 4796533 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 264m
Length 59m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 12th November 2002; 23rd December 2008; 18th September 2013; 11th October 2025

A 10m wide and 4m high cave entrance leads to a cow shed floor and past a column to a calcited chamber with a slope up at the rear. There is a shell midden in the entrance pointing to a Mesolithic occupation. A carbon deposit and black marks on the wall point to activity during the medieval period (Ruiz Cobo Jesús et al, 2008, p216).
A low section, walled off on the west side of the first chamber, was entered in 2013 and appeared to contain some old animal bones.
A small cave, to the right of the main entrance and entered up a short climb, has rotting metal containers. Tthe passage at the rear may dig as it appears to have a faint draught, although in 2025 it was thought to be "a very long term prospect with no draught".
A short narrow passage on the lower left of the second chamber could be a possible dig. This needs a thin person to have a look, but there is no draught.
