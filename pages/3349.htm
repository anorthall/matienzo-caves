This is a rift, first explored at Easter 2004, some 40m above and to the left of The Sewer. The opening, in a 1 x 2m limestone outcrop, drops  in a very narrow, sharp, spikey rift to a choked floor (about 20m?). A narrow rift continues back up the other side. There was no noticeable draught at Easter 2004. It was refound and GPS'd, Easter 2010.
