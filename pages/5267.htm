The information for this site has been shared by Guy Simonnot (Simonnot G, 2022) and roughly translated from the French using Google Translate as a starting point. The original grid reference used ED50 datum which has been converted to the ETRS89 datum above, with the altitude taken off the MCP QGIS map.

   The shaft is well identifiable thanks to a small tree that comes out of its entrance and the barbed wire that surrounds it. This 2 x 1.4 m hole consists of a single 9 m pitch with no continuation at the bottom. It must have served for a long time as a dumpster hole given the large amount of garbage found at the bottom.
