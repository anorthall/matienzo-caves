The GPS'd grid reference puts the site to the west of the previous position (ETRS89: 30T 448768 4794871) , more out in the open.

Both holes are above the streambed just below the southern radio-location point in Torcón de la Calleja Rebollo (258). The western hole is an 8m shaft to a run-in at the base with a draught from a rift. The second hole was dug through a squeeze to a choked base at 6m depth.

There are a couple of other holes well above at the small cliff line which need relocating and numbering.
