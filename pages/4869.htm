A cave which has archaeological interest. A short jungle search failed to find the entrance in April 2019 and in April 2025. The survey (below) suggests that the entrance is about 2m in height.

References: Ruiz Cobo Jesús and Muñoz Fernández Emilio et al, 2009 (survey); anon., 2019b (Easter logbook); anon., 2025a (Easter logbook)
