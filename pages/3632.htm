N Vega 30T 449514 4796301 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 348m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 29th November 2012

On a warm day this dig has a cold draught out. It lies between site 2661 and a barbed wire fence in the direction of site 0434. It has been partially dug into the top of a silted chamber. It really needs the whole mud bank digging away.

When visited in November, 2012, the site was described as a narrow draughting rift which needs capping.
