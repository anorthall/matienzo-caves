Note: This area of fenced shafts is grazed by cattle. The farmer has protected the animals by fencing, allowing vegetation and saplings to run wild around the shafts and piling in extra dead branches, etc. In August 2021 he was not happy with having these defences disturbed. A conversation is required to allow further investigations in the area.

   First documented as site X004.   An undescended, fenced shaft. Although a route was cleared to the shaft top on 1/8/21, the farmer was not happy (see above) and escorted the team off the area.
