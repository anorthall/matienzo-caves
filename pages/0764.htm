0764: Wet Dream Stream Passage
Seldesuto 30T 449018 4794971 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 204m
Length 102m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 8th June 1998

Excavated entrance leads to small streamway which splits, one branch heading towards Torcón de la Calleja Rebollo (258) and the other towards Cueva del Arenal (035). Both branches become too small. The site floods completely in wet weather but then dries up after 12 hours of no rain, suggesting that it is a flood resurgence.
