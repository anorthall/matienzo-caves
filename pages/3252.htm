Length 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 28th September 2012

Two entrances up off the track through vegatation; the left hand one is marked "CM". They were explored at Easter 2011 and found to be an 8m long, stooping/hands and knees remnant with soft calcite on the walls. There is also a "phreatic remnant visible near the base of a shakehole" about 100m south of this site.

Reference: anon., 2009a (Easter logbook); anon., 2011b (Easter logbook)
