   A few meters above the road to the Col de Alisas.
   A large entrance (10 x 3 m), surrounded by stakes and barbed wire, extending at its southern end by a pitch estimated at 6 or 7 m.
The opening is at the edge of Colorado sandstone with the top of the Vallina limestone.
