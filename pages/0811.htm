A large, circular shaft, first documented in 1990 and first descended 22 years later. The shaft is on the hillside overlooking the complex which contains site 298.

A 10m pitch (requiring 3 ladders?) lands in a large chamber which slopes up into the hill to another 10m pitch that chokes. Animal bones here are embedded in calcite.
