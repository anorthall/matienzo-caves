Length 43m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 1st February 2011; 21st March 2023

   A small, excavated entrance drops onto rubble that slopes down to a passage that parallels the hillside. Right is walking size to where it closes in; left is decorated hands and knees / crawling which also closes in. There is a flatout crawl at the back which rises into small phreatic pockets and rifts.
   The cave was surveyed in March 2023.
