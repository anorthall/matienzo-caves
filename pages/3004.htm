3004: Aldi, Torca
La Secada 30T 453312 4797966 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 342m
Length 64m Depth 13m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 25th October 2016

The excavated entrance is below the track and opposite a green bath. The 13m entrance shaft opens up into a fine, decorated chamber, 15 to 20m across with a ridge across the centre. One side goes down to a pool approximately 5 x 4m and very deep when there has been recent wet weather. It was thought to be a possible dive but when the hole was re-explored and surveyed in the summer, 2016, the pool was 1 x 1m and not deep. The entrance was also enlarged at that time.
