2451: cave
S Vega 30T 451931 4794477 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 554m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th May 2019

   A cave at the foot of a cliff which has a rock slope to a visible floor. Requires a thin person or a lump hammer.
    This was explored (?) in December 2018 and described as appearing undisturbed with no signs of previous exploration. A hading passage descends approximately 3 - 4m and leads to a small chamber with cobble/small boulder floor.
