Length 10m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

It is possible to drive up the track into the Los Pozos area and park at the end. On the right of the track, towards the highest point is a deep sink hole with steep sides. A 5m steep slither down then a 5m ladder reaches the base of the pit which is covered with cow bones and has no way on.
