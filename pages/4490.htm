4490: shaft
Navajeda 30T 444288 4800697 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 143m
Length 15m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 10th November 2021

   A fenced shaft covered with dense brambles. This is a 3m pitch to a boulder with a further climb down a slope to a mud-floored blockage. At the boulder area, a narrow rift appears to close down.
   In the wall, part way down the shaft, a dig over 3 days in September 2021 revealed a 7m pitch in a roomy shaft to a stand-up 4m x 2m chamber. There are good formations but no way on.
