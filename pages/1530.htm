Length 27 + 11m Depth 8 + 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 3rd April, 14th September 2021

   A large entrance above Cueva de los Cefrales. There are a number of holes around the entrance, all of which have been investigated. A scramble down into the main cave leads to a small hole onto a 4m climb down. At the base, a short crawl enters a small, choked chamber.
    A proximate site to the east has a shaft and cave entrance.
