Two choked shakeholes lie on the uphill side of a barbed wire fence. This shaft is on the other side and is stuffed with rusty barbed wire at the top. A 13m pitch past a jammed rock drops directly to the floor. There is a stoop under the north wall into the base of an aven. The southern wall has an low alcove with a sandstone run-in.
