0049: Somo, Torca del (2808 (French: SCD))
S Vega 30T 450888 4793881 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 670m
Length 139m Depth 77m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 8th November 2003; 14th May 2015; 17th June, 14th November 2022

The entrance is a 15m pitch over some precariously poised boulders to a ledge. Pitches of 9 and 7m then follow immediately, landing on a scree slope which descends to a narrow passage on the right.

After 15m and a couple of short climbs, the narrow and sharply fretted head of a 23m pitch is reached.

A 10m deep hole from the final chamber chokes, as does a large, rising boulder slope.
