Updated 20th September 2014

"Other entrance in field" near to site 1013 and beneath a level with a trough for cows. The site was explored in 2014 when rocks were removed from the entrance. A 4m crawl leads to a 2m climb down into a pool of cow shit. To the left a 90° bend enters a tight rift where a p4 is lined as the walls are "very crumbly for free climbing". A 1m climb up meets a large stal column and a flowstone covered rift. This continues to a breakdown chamber but no way on. All leads have been looked at, but the site has not been surveyed.

Reference: anon., 2007b (Easter logbook); anon., 2014c (summer logbook)
