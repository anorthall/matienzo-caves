   A long depression with a short rift at the eastern end.
    This was re-investigated in August 2021 and further described as having a hole at the base of the shakehole which closes off. A slot halfway down the northern end opened up to 2m horizontal passage with a 2x2m chamber at the end which closed up. A small hole at the base is too tight.
