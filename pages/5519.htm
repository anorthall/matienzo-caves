5519: resurgence
La Helguera 30T 451235 4802652 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 100m
Length 7m Depth 5m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   A dry river valley leads up to a rock face with a resurgence cave. A vertical climb down for 5 meters. The passage ahead is shoulder-width but closes down immediately with the stream running along the boulder-strewn floor. There is a narrow slot at head height with no draught. It may be worth checking for a draught on a warm day. The cave must emit a lot of water during floods. (There may be a photo?)
