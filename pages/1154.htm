Updated 6th November 2004; 10th October 2005

The entrance lies at the base of a steep-sided shakehole. A steeply descending loose tube above a stream sink leads to calcite and a boulder choke. (Nearby there appears to be another hole possibly mistaken for this one visited in October 2004, and described as an unexplored shallow shaft, 1m from the path, covered with rocks).
