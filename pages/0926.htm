A slope ends at a 12m pitch to a slope and ledge above a 36m pitch. The landing is in a large chamber with a scree slope up to the left which ends at a choke.

A climb down through boulders leads to another ledge and a very loose 17m pitch. The drop ends in a large chamber with a trickle of water disappearing under the back wall, blocked by sand.
