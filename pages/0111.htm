0111: shaft
La Secada 30T 452174 4798458 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 402m
Length 41m Depth 33m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 26th January, 9th June, 14th November 2025; 19th January 2026

   A 26m, tree-belayed pitch lands on a boulder slope which descends to a choke. The site could not be found at the end of 2024, but a nearby new site 5458 was thought to be a candidate for a short time.  The 1981 photos appear to show the hole nearer the road than indicated on the map.
   Five difficult searches through dense cover failed to reveal the whereabouts of the shaft in October / November 2025. A drone flight over the area also failed to reveal any definite black holes.
   The site was eventually found in dense vegetation in December 2025 then re-explored and surveyed to confirm the original description. The GPS'd position is just 16m from the original guessed coordinates but the search area was an almost impenetrable jungle.
