Las Calzadillas 30T 447125 4795009 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 550m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   A small hole with a good outward cold draught in a small depression containing a decent sized tree at one side. With a spade, an earth rift was opened up between 1 and 2m deep, appearing to run back under the small depression. It has been covered and left for a future visit with more people and gear. [Site 13b; Phil Parker]
