Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 24th April 2013; 20th September 2014; 9th May 2018

   An excavated, draughting, bouldery hole above a wet weather stream sink in a wooded shakehole. Further work was carried out at Easter 2013 and in the summer 2014 when it was left with scaffolding required.
   A couple of days were spent removing more material over Easter 2018. The site now requires warmer weather to trace the draught.
