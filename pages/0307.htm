0307: cave
S Vega 30T 451115 4795420 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 235m
Length 8m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated September 17th 2000; 20th December 2008; 17th September 2014

A small, easily missed entrance leads to a 1.5m high room with a crazy paving floor and small chamber to the right.

The cave could have been used as a shelter during the Civil War as there are various rusty items on the floor. Further illustrated information about the Civil War in the area can be found here.
