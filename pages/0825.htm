0825: Coquisera II, Cueva de
S Vega 30T 452661 4794352 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 485m
Length 35m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 4th October 2006; 23rd December 2008; 2nd November 2009; 12th September 2021; 20th June 2022

An obvious open hole next to a tree on the left side of the valley running up to Cueva de la Coquisera (039). Passage goes 30m through a couple of small chambers and a flat out crawl to a muddy choke in a rift. There is Bronze Age pottery where the passage first turns to the left.
