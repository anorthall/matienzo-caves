Updated 29th November 2012

The entrance lies above and to the right of site 3615. A 10m long cave sloped down to a blockage which was removed in November 2012 to reveal a 3m slope down to a calcited, small chamber. There was no obvious way on through the floor and no draught was noted. A crawl / inlet on the left chokes.
