A p4 is rigged off a tree to a choked floor. A slide into a short passage leads to a 3m climb up and a p5 (or a free-climb with a rope) into a decent-sized chamber which is blind at the base. A possible lead across the top of the p5 (at the same level as the entrance) requires bolts.
