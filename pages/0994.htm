A sink which chokes after 8m with flood debris. In summer 2006, it was noted that at the end of the "8m" was a window into another visible 6 or 7m, and a dig through would be easy. At Easter 2007 a "good size" stream was seen flowing into the entrance. The site was video'd in 2013.
