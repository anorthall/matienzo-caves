5321: shaft
S Vega 30T 451396 4795364 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 223m
Length 21m Depth 11m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

   A scaffold pole backed-up to a tree was the top belay for the first descent.
    The drop passes through formations and water-worn calcite. There are skeletal remains of a dog at the base of the shaft and the skeletal remains of a goat (with ear tag) in a small alcove chamber at the base. There is very little horizontal passage but a mud and calcite blockage was broke open to reveal a short section of passage that ends in a clean-washed, head-and-shoulders sized aven with a small amount of water washing down the wall.
