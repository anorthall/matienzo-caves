This hole is not far from site 134 and has a small Yew tree in entrance. A drop of about 5m reaches a ledge then 15m reaches the floor. A 4m climb down follows a rift to the left which ends in a calcite wall with a possible climb up at the top. It is probably not worth attempting this "although a splash was heard on the other side".
