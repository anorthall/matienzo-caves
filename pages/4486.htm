Updated 27th June 2022

A well known cave in the Riotuerto district which was explored and surveyed by the Speleo Club Cántabro in 2009. The entrance lies uphill of the La Riega resurgence. (In the autumn 2016, the resurgence was the scene of a successful water trace from a Moncobe hole (5182) being explored by the GE Pistruellos.)
