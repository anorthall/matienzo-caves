A site with 2 shaft entrances in the same opening. To the right is a slope to the head of a 15m pitch landing on a calcite and gravel slope with a 3m climb down. The left hand shaft is shorter and lands on a boulder slope. Stones can be thrown through a slot to connect with the previous shaft.
