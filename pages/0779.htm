El Naso 30T 451458 4796531 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 472m
Length 12m Depth 12m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 12th November 2002; 20th May 2026

A hole in a shallow shakehole which draughts in. A 3m climb leads to a crawl into a small chamber. Straight ahead becomes too small, while another climb drops into a lower chamber. At the lowest point reached the draught is lost but there is another route which hasn't been followed.

A visit in 2026 describes an inward draught, 2 x 2m shafts and loose boulders at a 3rd chamber. The site didn't look promising. (Video link below.)
