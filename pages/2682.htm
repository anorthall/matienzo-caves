   A goat pen to the east of La Cuevuca, site 177. A 6m wide entrance is 2m high and the passage goes in 12m. The back of the cave is blocked by an oil drum but there is no draught.
   The relative positions of the 3 sites to the southeast can be seen here.

   There was no sign of any bat activity when visited in April 2023.
