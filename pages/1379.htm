1379: cave (Candy's Dig)
La Secada 30T 451289 4797227 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 288m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 14th May 2000; 21st October 2001; 18th September 2013; 21st May 2026

The entrance is to the south of a small bluff and is covered with one of Pablo's signs. The cave descends to a rift with boulders and a good draught. A good dig.
When revisited in 2013, there was a boulder over the top and the strong draught felt at the top was not felt at the bottom.

When seen in April 2026, it was described as looking, "loose and would need considerable effort to stabilise. Pablo's sign covering it was very decrepit."
