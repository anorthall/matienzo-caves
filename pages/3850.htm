Length 10m Depth 4m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 6th May 2016

This hole above the track was excavated at Easter 2016. A slither through and drop down lands on a slope into a small choked chamber with stal. A few metres to the southeast is a choked hole which may connect, although this was not inspected at Easter 2016.
