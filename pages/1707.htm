Updated 22nd January 2013

This small hole is in a wooded shakehole on the edge of a newly (Xmas 2001) terraformed area. It can be seen that a body-sized drop continues down beyond 8m deep. On a visit in December 2012, the hole appeared to be blocked up after 1m with a large, loose boulder restricting access.
Ten metres out from the wooded hole are a couple of holes broken into by a bulldozer. They appear rather immature and chossy.

Reference: anon, 2001d (Christmas logbook); anon., 2012f (Christmas logbook)
