Riaño 30T 452037 4798988 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: A) Altitude 325m
Length 20m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance is a crawl in a scar. From a 3 x 2m chamber turn left into a larger chamber (3 x 6 - 8m). From here there are 2 small inlets. There is old calcite in abundance and a choked bedding heads off into the hill. No draught.
