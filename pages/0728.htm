Length 25m Depth 25m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance is on the hill which contains Cueva de los Emboscados (087). A 5m pitch leads to a boulder floor and a traverse in a rift. Descended in three places: the far end for a few feet before it becomes too tight; a second which becomes too tight after 5m and a third which drops for 20 - 25m in a choked rift with the draught disappearing into the boulder floor.

A mucky, no-hoper.
