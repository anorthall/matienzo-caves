0402: shaft
La Gatuna 30T 449948 4799371 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: M) Altitude 218m
Length 31m Depth 26m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 24th April 2005

A short jungle-bash to a vegetated depression with a small hole. The loose entrance funnels down to a nice drop through alternate sandstone and limestone beds. At the base is a 5m high chamber with the odd formation but no outlet.

In 2005, the entrance was not found. It may have collapsed and this site is documented as 2250.

References: anon., 1982 (logbook); Corrin J, 1983c; anon., 2005b (Easter & summer)
