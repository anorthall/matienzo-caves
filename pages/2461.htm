Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 13th May 2007; 8th January 2020

A stooping entrance in a shallow depression down off the track. A 14m long chamber closes in at the rear with a possible dig. The cave is an animal lair.
The cave has been documented by the ADEMCO as AD-62 on p35-37 in the ADEMCO 2019 Journal.

Reference: anon., 2006b (Easter logbook); anon., 2007b (Easter logbook); Sierra Jose Luis, 2019
