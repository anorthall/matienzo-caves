Cobadal 30T 448917 4798235 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 253m
Length 15m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 2nd November 2010

A walk-in entrance at the top of the field above the house. A chamber stretches back to a walled crawl that enters a small phreatic pocket where a possible, long term dig stretches off. On the left of the entrance chamber, near the back, a small passage leads off that hasn't been pushed due to the presence of a bat. On the same side, closer to the entrance, a tiny alcove had a few stones removed to reveal a small cache of bullets, possibly stored in an accompanying rusty "pepper pot" which had fragments of newspaper. These remains were given to the house owner.
