    A cave in a small depression, heading diagonally for maybe 7m, quite roomy but ending at a small hole into a rising boulder choke/run-in of 2m length. Next to the entrance a floor level wriggle into a small chamber gives another 2m of passage. [Phil Parker]

   In November 2023, the site was described as a shelf cave ending at dead-end, low chamber.
