A cave in the middle of thick, prickly jungle that is easier to approach from above. The cave lies below a small cliff on the Matienzo side of the westerly ridge from Cruz Uzano. The 1 x 1m entrance is leads to a 1m high, 2m wide passage that ends after 10m at a sediment and calcite choke.
