3332: Bendito Pantin, Torca
Llueva 30T 454973 4798427 (Datum: ETRS89. Accuracy code: G) Altitude 197m
Length 12m Depth 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 7th October 2012; 24th June 2022

This hole is high above the road at a wide bend that used to contain a depression, now filled with road-works material. The tight, oval hole has been explored down a tight shaft to a depth of 10m by ADEMCO in 2010. The site was, and may still be, partly covered by dissected lump of limestone. Marked AD-2.
