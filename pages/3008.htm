An open entrance at the top of a depression just inside the woods. Stooping height passage leads to a stal boss at a left hand turn. An attempt seems to have been made to extract this feature as there is a trench around it. The passage then swings back on the original joint direction with walking over blocks. The route tightens at a stal blockage where a crawl on the right meets a run-in. Unsurveyed.
