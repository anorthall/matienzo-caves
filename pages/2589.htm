Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

The entrance is in clints, above and to the east of the wood. A small rift lies on the north side of a small depression with a 5m high oak tree. A 2m drop leads to a narrow rift which requires enlarging to a 2-3m drop. The rift appears to continue bigger. There is a good inward draught and it is worth spending a day digging.
