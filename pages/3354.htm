Length 10m
Area position : Site entrance in context : Logbook search

Updated 3rd. 16th May 2012

The entrance is about 16m to the SE and 4m higher than the entrance to Cueva Llueva (site 0114). A slope up reaches a low-arched passage 80cm wide and 40-50cm high that continues for about 10m to a mud and calcite choke. This point is about 15m from a passage in Cueva Llueva.
