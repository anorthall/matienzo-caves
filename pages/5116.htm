Updated 24th May 2022

   This shaft was descended in autumn 2019, but written up 2 years later. The grid reference in approximate as it has not been GPS'd or the position clearly remembered.
    Re-explored Oct 2019. It is in the western end of a copse entered by a 3m drop and then 6m rope down a treed slope to a newly bolted Y-hang above a large 10m wide shaft. Near the Y-hang on the rock face are a set of old spits. From the Y-hang a 31m drop leads to an 8m boulder slope into a large 15m wide chamber that leads, in turn, to a short alcove at the back but with no way on. (Length and depth are guestimates.)
